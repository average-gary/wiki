# Blue Ridge Meats: Frederick County incumbent processor terms

Pages fetched 2026-10-02: home page, /customer-information-1, /cut-sheets (plus the beef cut-sheet PDF), and the blog post "custom-butchering-and-meat-processing-for-farmers" (dated September 16, year not shown). "Blue Ridge Meats pricing is subject to change without notice." **None of these pages lists a kill fee or a $/lb processing rate.**

## Facts
- Identity: "USDA Meat Processing | Blue Ridge Meats | Front Royal" (site title). Address on the customer page: **2391 Guard Hill Rd, Middletown, VA 22645**. Phone (540) 636-6050; email lois@blueridgemeats.com.
- Self-description: "USDA Inspected Facility", "USDA Custom Processing", "Your Neighborhood Abattoir", "Full-service Meat processing." [inferred: an on-site kill floor plus cut-and-wrap.]
- **Booking lead time: "We book 3 to 5 months in advance."** This is direct evidence of a slaughter-slot bottleneck in the Winchester/Frederick County market in 2026.
- Fees: "you will be charged a $50 no-show fee", and the next appointment must then be paid upfront. "You will have 72 hours before a storage fee will apply. The storage fee will be $10 per day per cart used." Credit cards are charged a 3.5% convenience fee; a returned check costs $50. Payment is due at pickup.
- Species with cut sheets: beef (whole or half), pork (whole hog), lamb/goat (whole).

## Beef cut sheet (PDF) — operational details
- Header fields: "USDA RESALE ____ NOT FOR SALE ____", "TAG#", "HOT WT", "HARVEST DATE", "DESIRED HANG TIME: ____ DAYS", "WHOLE / HALF". So the plant runs both inspected-for-resale and custom (not-for-sale) beef, and bills on hot (hanging) weight [inferred from the HOT WT field].
- Value-added options: fresh sausage, smoked sausage, summer sausage, frankfurters, snack sticks and burger patties, each with a **25 lb minimum** per flavor. "* Extra charges may apply."
- "All processing fees are subject to, but not limited to collection fees, late fees, storage fees and attorney fees."
