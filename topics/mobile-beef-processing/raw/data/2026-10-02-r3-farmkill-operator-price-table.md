## Source notes

### Backyard Butchery, "2025 Pricing" (Harrah, Oklahoma)
Read from the operator's own pricing page. The page header reads "On-site Meat Processing - We Come To You". Contact details on the page: (405) 887-3066, freelandfarms@cox.net, Tiffany@BackyardButchery.com. These are the beef and bison lines, verbatim:
- "Deposit $100.00"
- "Slaughter Fee - under 1800# $100"
- "Slaughter Fee Over 1800# $150"
- "Hanging Weight* $0.90/lb", with the note "*Not to be confused with Live Weight. Configure live weight minus 50% for hanging weight on cattle."
- "Milage $2.65/mile Configured one way from 73045"
- "DIsposal Fee $100 If we haul off the inedibles"
- "Emergency Appointment $200.00"
- "Daily Minimum $500.00"

The pricing structure separates three things: a flat kill fee, a per-mile travel charge, and an optional offal haul-off fee. Leaving offal on the farm is the default; haul-off is an add-on. [inferred]

Worked example [inferred, arithmetic only]: one 1,300 lb steer 30 miles from base costs $100 kill + $79.50 mileage + $100 disposal (if hauled), which is $279.50 before cutting. Because of the $500 daily minimum, a single-head call is billed at no less than $500.

### NMPAN, "Central Coast CA Mobile Harvest Unit"
An NMPAN case-study page with no date on the page. It describes a USDA-inspected MPU built with $138,750 in redirected federal economic development funds; Bruce Dunlop, who built the Washington unit, built this one. It is included for comparison only, because it is USDA-inspected, not custom-exempt. Quotes:
- "Price of services: prices vary. Operational costs: $800+ per day without overtime, more for locations >50 mi. distant"
- "the unit has to carry the offal back to the cut & wrap, in 44 gallon Rubbermaid tubs, to be picked up by the local rendering company. The fee, ~$6/animal, is fairly cheap, but the whole process requires extra staff time"
- "With a $150/head kill fee, and the cost of building a ranch site, small growers ... may not be able to make on[-ranch slaughter work]"

## What was searched and not found (Mid-Atlantic / Appalachia)
- Web search: DuckDuckGo (HTML, lite, POST, d.js), Bing HTML and RSS (only the first query token is honoured, so results were irrelevant), Brave, Mojeek, Yahoo, AOL, Ask, Startpage, Ecosia, Qwant, Yandex, MetaGer, Google via WebFetch and Reddit JSON were all blocked or useless. mwmbl returned a few results; none were operator price pages.
- WVDA Licensed Custom Establishments list (21 Sep 2026): no entry is marked mobile, and no prices are given (already covered in round 1).
- NCDA plant directory and the PA, KY and MD agriculture department pages: no mobile custom list with prices was reachable. The Penn State and UMD extension processor-list URLs return 404; WVU Extension returns 403.
- Butcher Bud (butcherbud.com/mobile-slaughter) is a scraped directory that claims "789 mobile slaughter and on-farm meat processing services", including VA 30, WV 9, MD 21, PA 27, NC 25, TN 18, KY 17 and OH 30. Its "mobile" label is unreliable: most VA entries are fixed plants (T&E Meats, Seven Hills, Gore's, Blue Ridge Meats). Listings carry no website or price. Mid-Atlantic names that may really be on-farm or mobile and are worth a phone call: **Gentle Harvest Custom Processing (Winchester, VA)**, Mountain Man Custom Butchering LLC (Orrstown, PA), Homestead Butchering (Richland, PA; its Squarespace site has expired). gentleharvestcustomprocessing.com is a parked domain.
- Valley Mobile Slaughter (formerly Dale's Mobile Slaughter; Wallowa, Union and Baker counties, OR; 541-263-0459) has a website but publishes no prices.
