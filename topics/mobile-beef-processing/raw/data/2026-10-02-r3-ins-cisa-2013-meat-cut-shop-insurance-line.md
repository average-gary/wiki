# CISA Meat Processing Business Template (2013): insurance line items

Files used:
- Workbook: https://www.buylocalfood.org/upload/resource/CISA.Meat.Processing.Business.Template.2013.xlsx
- Assumptions PDF: https://www.buylocalfood.org/upload/resource/CISA.MeatProcessingFacility.CashFlowTemplateReferences2013.pdf

NMPAN links to it from https://www.nichemeatprocessing.org/business-planning-resources/.

## Modeled business (assumptions PDF and Revenue sheet)

- **Services:** cutting and vacuum packing only. The fee benchmarks come from "an informal survey of 6 USDA-inspected slaughter and processing facilities" in the Northeast. Slaughter is not modeled.
- **Fees:** $0.85/lb hanging weight for beef and hogs, $1.00/lb for sheep and goats. Value-added items: ground beef $0.20/lb, smoked beef $2.00/lb, linked sausage $2.00/lb, smoked pork $1.25/lb.
- **Throughput:** 3 beef (671 lb average hanging), 9 hogs, and 2 sheep or goats per week, for 50 weeks. Labor is 8 h per beef, 2.5 h per hog, and 1.5 h per sheep or goat.
- **Staff:** 2 FTE. Manager/cutter at $25/h ($52,000) and assistant manager/cutter at $18/h ($37,440), for $89,440 of direct wages.
- **Space:** 2,500 sq ft leased at $6/sq ft ($15,000) plus a triple-net charge of $1/sq ft ($2,500) "for taxes, insurance, etc." The notes say: "If plan is to purchase a facility, then costs for taxes and insurance should be adjusted."
- **Revenue:** $192,306 in Year 1 at 85% capacity, rising to $226,243 from Year 3 at 100%.

## Insurance figures

| Line (Operating Costs sheet) | Basis | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Insurance | $800/month x 12 | **$9,600** (flat in Years 1-6) |
| Employer taxes & workers comp | 15% of direct salary | $13,416 |
| Employee benefits (health etc.) | 15% of salary | $13,416 |
| Triple-net lease add-on (building taxes and insurance) | $1/sq ft x 2,500 | $2,500 |

- Source note for the insurance line: "Estimate based on conversations with existing processors."
- The workers' comp note: "Employer taxes (Social Security, Medicare, Workers Compensation Insurance) were calculated at 15% of salary." Workers' comp is **not** broken out. FICA alone is 7.65% [inferred], which leaves roughly 7.35% of payroll at most for workers' comp and unemployment combined.
- The template does not split the $9,600 into general liability, product liability, property, or auto. It has no vehicle (the shop has no kill truck) and no spoilage or bailee line shown separately.

## Derived ratios [inferred]

- Insurance is about 4.2% of steady-state gross revenue ($9,600 / $226,243) and about 5.9% of total operating costs ($9,600 / $162,272).
- In 2013 dollars. Commercial P&C pricing has risen substantially since then, so a 2026 Virginia figure would likely be higher. That needs a current quote.
- For the Virginia mobile plan, add commercial auto for the tow truck and trailer, inland marine for the MSU trailer, and kill-floor workers' comp exposure (code 2081). None of these is in this template.
