# NMPAN Small Meat Processors Business Planning Guide (May 2011): MSU section, read contrarian

NMPAN's MSU Financials page (https://www.nichemeatprocessing.org/msu-financials/) says the guide "has a section with real financial information from a real mobile slaughter unit operating under USDA inspection in California," and that the guide "is being updated in 2023." The figures are 2011-2014 projections, not actuals. Credibility is scored 3 for that reason.

## Framing in the guide (p. 27-28)

- "MSUs have been around, as custom-exempt operations, for a long time ... However, USDA- and state-inspected MSUs are relatively new. The first USDA-inspected MSU began operations only 10 years ago" (i.e., about 2001-2002).
- The guide lists four configurations: an existing processor adds an MSU in place of a kill floor; a producer group builds an MSU and partners with an existing cut-and-wrap; the same group builds both; a large ranch builds both for itself.
- Questions every MSU plan must answer: **Ownership** (same or different entities, "formal and fiscal relationship"); **Cut and wrap** ("If not, how will the cut and wrap aspect of processing be addressed?"); **Offal/water disposal** ("If the MSU is required to collect and dispose of the waste water/offal off-site, how and where?"); **Capacity** ("maximum amount of livestock that can be processed in a given time based on available inspection hours and travel time").

## Start-up summary (excludes cut-and-wrap)

Mobile slaughter unit $200,000; tractor truck (used) $26,000; unit equipment $5,000; cell/laptop $680; DMV $1,200; insurance $1,200; USDA stamp $350; organic cert $350; licenses $300; office $200; hide tags $30. **Total $235,310.**

## Pro forma results

| Year | Sales | Total expenses | Net profit (after tax) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011 | $191,506 | $142,992 | $36,385 |
| 2012 | $215,794 | $137,383 | $58,808 |
| 2013 | $243,162 | $131,369 | $83,845 |
| 2014 | $302,561 | $127,950 | $130,958 |

Flat annual lines: salaries $72,000; payroll taxes $10,800; fuel $12,000; depreciation $12,300; maintenance $7,200; gut disposal $6,000 ($500/month); insurance $2,400; DMV $1,200; BIT program $300. Interest falls from $15,042 (2011) to $0 (2014).

## Assumptions (p. 35), quoted

- "Unit Volume is based on the number of animals that can be processed through the unit with two full-time butchers in an eight-hour period, 260 workdays per year."
- Staff: BLS May 2009 animal slaughtering mean wage of $12.95/hr ($26,930/yr). "MSU butchers require a commercial driver's license, hence the increase in salary base." Payroll expense is 15%.
- "If the MSU is running as an addition to a cut and wrap facility, legal, accounting, and other consultants would be included in that facility's costs. If a MSU is running as a stand-alone business, these services would be added."
- Equipment financing is "generally easier to obtain" because the unit is collateral.

## Contrarian stress points [inferred]

1. **Utilization is the whole model.** Revenue assumes 260 full kill days a year from launch, growing every month. Rusted Gate (2022, separate file) projected 1-2 days a week. Nevada's UNR feasibility study (2007, via NMPAN) assumed 240 days per unit and found fall-heavy demand that needed calving shifts or variable pricing to flatten. NMPAN notes of the two MSUs plus stationary fabrication facility it modeled: "None of it was built."
2. **Hidden subsidy from the cut-and-wrap.** Marketing, rent, utilities, benefits, and stand-alone admin are $0, because the guide assumes the fixed facility carries them. A stand-alone MSU or producer coop must add these.
3. **Labor priced at 2009 wages, no benefits.** Two butchers at about $36K each with CDLs and no benefits is unrealistic for a 2026 Virginia hire. Rusted Gate flagged butcher scarcity as a top threat.
4. **Offal at $500/month** assumes a renderer or disposal route exists. California prohibits on-ranch composting (see the MSU manual file). Virginia's route is unverified.
5. **Unit price drift.** $200,000 in 2011 compares with roughly $550,000 total build-out for a turnkey Friesla unit in 2022 (Rusted Gate). That figure includes start-up items, so it is not a like-for-like comparison.
