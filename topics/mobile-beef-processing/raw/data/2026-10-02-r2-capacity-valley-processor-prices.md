## T&E Meats, "2025 PROCESSING FEES" (PDF; pork and lamb pages headed "2024")

Beef:
- Beef kill fee: $75 each
- Weigh and label for re-sale: $0.10/lb
- Standard cut and pack: $1.05/lb
- Boneless chuck cut and pack (chuck broken into premium steaks): $1.15/lb
- Restaurant cut and pack (sub-primals, all whole cuts): $0.95/lb
- Quartering (split or front/hind): $0.25/lb
- Cut into primals (8 pieces): $0.35/lb
- Collect and pack organs: $1.25/lb
- "Mad Cow" handling (beef over 30 months): $50/head
- Dry-age beyond two weeks: $25 per head per week

Further processing:
- Vacuum-packed ground beef: $0.20/lb (1-2 lb), $0.05/lb (5-10 lb)
- Lean grind: $0.40/lb
- Stew/kabob: $0.60/lb
- Patties: $1.50/lb
- Seasoned sausage: $1.00/lb (50 lb minimum per flavor)
- Beef bacon, celery-cure: $3.50/lb green weight

Pork (2024 header): kill $60; large hog (400+ lb hanging) $80; cut and pack $1.00/lb; kill and scald $115/hog.

The Services page says beef hangs "one to two weeks". Pricing is "typically reviewed and updated annually."

[inferred] Worked example: a 750 lb hanging-weight steer under 30 months with standard cut and USDA resale labels costs $75 + 750 × ($1.05 + $0.10) = **$937.50** before extras. Without resale labels (custom/locker) it costs $75 + 750 × $1.05 = **$862.50**.

## Seven Hills Food, 2026 USDA beef cut sheet and delivery sheet (PDFs)

- "1 head or 100 | No lead times | No 'fees' | Flexible schedules | Delivery | Free boxes | Premium packaging."
- Tiered price × carcass weight: 1-2 head $1.30/lb; 3-7 head $1.25/lb; 8+ head $1.20/lb. "$775 carcass minimum." "NO kill or disposal fees."
- "Harvest is scheduled for the day after drop off." "Carcasses are hung in a rotation for 7-14 days +/-. Extended aging can be facilitated depending on available rail space."
- "Turn around time 3 - 4 weeks +/- after harvest." Cut sheet must be in by drop-off, or a $100 late fee applies. Freezing is "NOT standard"; product is delivered fresh, with a 14-28 day shelf life below 35°F.
- Grind in 1 lb bricks: $0.25/lb without a USDA label, $0.35/lb with one. Patties: $0.75/$1.00/lb. Organs: $1.50/lb.
- Packing animals separately costs $25 each; packing halves separately costs $50.
- Refrigerated delivery, 1-2 pallets (up to 2,500 lb): $225 for 0-50 mi, rising to $370 for 131-150 mi and $590 for 231-250 mi. Third-party blast freezing costs $50 to deliver pallets, $50 to pick up, and $25 per pallet per week.
- Client cited: "Primal Beef Company, Shenandoah Valley, VA", a national e-commerce brand using Virginia cattle.

[inferred] Worked example: one 750 lb carcass costs 750 × $1.30 = **$975** all-in. That is near T&E's labeled price ($937.50). So Valley USDA processing benchmarks at about $1.15-1.30/lb hanging weight all-in in 2025-26. A mobile or custom-exempt service would be priced against that.
