## Other county context (2022)
- Frederick: 94,314 acres in farms (-14%), and total market value of products sold $50.6M (+50%). Crops make up 89% of sales and livestock 11%, because the county is dominated by orchards.
- Clarke: total sales $16.4M. Livestock is 56% of sales.
- Shenandoah: total sales $224.7M. Livestock is 86% of sales (largely poultry).
- Warren: total sales $8.7M. Livestock is 70% of sales.
- Rockingham: total sales $1,197.1M, the largest in Virginia. Livestock is 94% of sales.
- Augusta: total sales $448.3M. Livestock is 89% of sales, and broiler inventory is 5,571,022.

## Caveats
- "Cattle and calves" includes beef cows, dairy cows, and young stock. Rockingham and Augusta have large dairy sectors, so their beef-relevant inventory is meaningfully lower than the headline. The county profiles do not separate beef cows. That split would come from Census Table 11 or Quick Stats ("Cattle, cows, beef - inventory") [not fetched].
- The direct-to-consumer column counts all products, not just beef.
- [inferred] For a mobile unit based near Winchester, the immediate service area (Frederick, Clarke, Warren) holds about 26,300 head. Shenandoah adds about 32,000. Reaching Rockingham and Augusta, 60 to 100 miles south, would bring the total to about 232,000.
