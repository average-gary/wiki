## Virginia rules relevant to mobile operators
Source: VDACS OMPS, "A Guide to Red Meat Custom Exemptions", https://www.vdacs.virginia.gov/pdf/custom-exemptions.pdf (fetched 2026-10-02).
- "The Office of Meat & Poultry Services (OMPS) issues Custom Permits to qualified operators. Permits are valid from the date of issue until June 30 of each year. There is no fee for the permit."
- "All Custom exempt operators are required to have a valid permit." The exception is operators holding a Virginia State or Federal Grant of Inspection.
- Item 7: "**A custom exempt operation may use a mobile slaughter and processing unit.**"
- Slaughter and processing can be split. The owner "may opt to have their livestock slaughtered under the custom exemption by one custom exempt operator, and then choose to have a second custom exempt operator do the processing." The owner may also slaughter the animal and take the carcass to a custom processing facility.
- "Field-dressed livestock (cattle, sheep, swine, and goats) may be brought in for custom exempt processing." The operator may ask for a written statement that the animal was ambulatory at slaughter.
- Recordkeeping under 9 CFR 320.1 applies. Inedible material and SRMs (specified risk materials) must be controlled to prevent diversion into food (9 CFR 303.1, 416.2(b)(4), 416.3(c)).
- How carcasses reach a cut shop [inferred from the items above]: a mobile custom slaughterer kills on the owner's farm, and the field-dressed carcass then goes to a separate custom-exempt processing facility for hanging and cutting. That legal handoff is what makes a "kill-only mobile + partner cut shop" model work in Virginia.
