# Beaverhead Meats (Dillon, MT): posted custom-exempt fees

## Business model
- "Since 1983, family-owned and operated. We are a state licensed, custom exempt meat shop. We provide custom processing of meat products for home consumption." Address: 620 N Montana St, Dillon, MT 59725.
- "Beaverhead Meats provides custom processing of domestic and wild animals. Our services include mobile slaughter, shipping, and special orders."
- The site says the crew is sometimes away "processing animals in the cutting room or out of the shop for domestic animal butchering". The mobile kill crew and the cut room share the same staff. [inferred]
- Equipment: Sentinel Trailers quotes "Beaverhead Meats, Dillon, MT" on its home page, fetched 2026-10-02: "Our custom-built slaughter trailer from Sentinel Trailers is a reliable workhorse! ... allows us to maximize our local ranch and farm-based slaughtering operation for domestic animals." See the Sentinel price file `2026-10-02-r2-rig-sentinel-slaughter-trailer-prices.md`. The custom-exempt Sentinel models cost $72k-$115k list in 2026.
- Booking: "We have partnered with Farmshare to schedule harvest dates and fill out cuts sheets online" (partners.farmshare.co/scheduling/beaverhead-meats-dillon-mt).

## Posted price list (website as of 2026-10-02; regulatory model: custom-exempt; Montana)

| Service | Posted price |
|---|---|
| Domestic animal processing (cut and wrap) | "$.86 to $1.00 per pound" |
| Kill charge (domestic) | "A separate kill charge of $75.00 to $125.00 will be charged when applicable" |
| Skinning, grinding, extra services | "$30.00 to $80.00" ("Skinning prices depend on species") |
| Wild game processing | $1.25-$2.00/lb, $75 minimum |
| Sausage, salami, jerky | $2.20-$4.50/lb |
| Storage after 10-day pickup window (game) | $10.00 per week |

Notes:
- The page does not say which weight basis the $/lb applies to. Custom shops usually charge on hanging (carcass) weight. [inferred]
- The site does not publish a separate mobile travel or mileage fee. It also does not say whether the $75-$125 kill charge covers an on-farm kill or only a kill at the shop.
- Payment "is due when services are rendered".

## Relevance to a Shenandoah Valley plan
This is a single data point from a Montana custom-exempt operator with a low rural price level. For the 2026 budget model, it implies roughly [inferred]:
- kill revenue of $75-$125 per head;
- cut-and-wrap revenue of about $520-$600 on a 600-lb hanging carcass.

Most of the revenue for an integrated "kill plus cut" custom operator comes from cutting, not from the kill fee. A kill-only mobile operator in Virginia would need a much higher kill fee, or a travel fee, to cover a $72k-$115k rig. This should be checked against Virginia/Maryland operator prices, which were not verified in this session because search was blocked.
