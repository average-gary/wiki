# Turnkey mobile cut-and-pack units and modular aging coolers (vendor specs, 2026)

## 1. Sentinel Trailers #5: 53 ft semi (pages fetched 2026-10-02)
- "Starting at $300,000" (2026 list). The trailer is "USDA Compliant". It is 53 ft long with a 10 ft ceiling and "18 ft Kill Compartment, 10 ft Refrigeration and 18 ft Butcher Compartment".
- **Butcher floor:** "20 ft Poly Top Heavy-duty Tables", "Commercial 3 Tub Sink", "Meat Grinder", "Meat Band Saw", "Vacum Sealer", man door, hand truck ramp, hose reel and hot water connection, "Ceiling Mounted Winch and Rail system", and "Shelves for Wrapping and Labeling".
  - On /trailer-5 the tables, grinder, saw, sealer and shelves carry an asterisk: "*Additional cost for upgrade".
  - The /custom-made-trailers page specifies a "10 hp Meat Grinder" and a "5hp Meat Band Saw".
- **Refrigeration:** a single 24,000 BTU unit with digital control and "Ability to Cool from 0 or 34 Degrees". The cooler compartment is 10 ft long, so carcass holding capacity is small [inferred]. Sentinel gives no head count for #5.
- **Utilities aboard:** a 35,000 W diesel generator in a front mechanical room. A "Transfer Switch Between Line Power and Generator", so the unit can run on shore power at a docking site [inferred from the switch].
  - Water: 200 gal freshwater tank, dual on-demand water heaters and dual 12 V pumps at 55 psi.
  - Drain: "Rear Drain System to 60 gallon Capacity with RV Drain".
  - Other: 220 V and 110 V outlets, triple 10,000 lb axles, 4 in spray urethane insulation.
- Sentinel also lists "Micro coolers" and "Refrigerated trailers" on /other-trailers, with no specs or prices.

## 2. Mobile Processing Trailers & Supply: turnkey trailers
Source: http://www.mobileprocessingplant.com/turnkey_processing_trailers.html (fetched 2026-10-02). The mobileprocessingtrailers.com domain is still a "Launching Soon" placeholder.
- The vendor pitch: "Fully equipped with all the tools and supplies needed to start operations immediately", "Ample cooler and freezer space", "Streamlined workflow for harvesting, chilling, cutting, and packaging."
- Quote-form classes:
  - Turnkey gooseneck, 38-44 ft: cooler capacity 4-8 beef carcasses.
  - Turnkey semi, 45 ft: 9.
  - Gooseneck or semi with freezer, 44-45 ft: 5.
  - Modular units, 38-48 ft x 11.5 ft wide x 12 ft: 6-16.
- **Equipment checklist** on the form: Breaking Table, Work Tables, Bandsaw, Grinder, Mixer, Stuffer, Vacuum Packer, Label Printing, Scales, Generator, Mini Split, Well Saw.
- The form asks "Number of days carcasses will be chilled in this unit". So the vendor expects some buyers to age carcasses onboard, and others to hold them elsewhere [inferred].
- **No prices published**; every unit is built to a quote. Contact (720) 626-5180.
- Financing page: commercial financing, plus a referral to the Flower Hill Institute for help finding USDA grants.

## 3. Friesla (Everson, WA; (800) 385-3390): modular systems
Pages: friesla.com/equipment/{cut-package-modules, coolers-freezers, preowned-systems, meat-processing-systems}, fetched 2026-10-02. **No prices published.**
- **Cut and Package Module:** built on a steel frame "to handle, cut, and package chilled animal carcasses on your site. Whether your animals were slaughtered at a large facility or on your farm."
  - Equipment is "specified during your system's Project Design Phase", "ranging from band saws and grinders to vacuum packers and rollstock packaging machines".
  - Photos show a stainless bandsaw, a Reiser HP1L vacuum filler, a Reiser Supervac double-chamber vacuum sealer, a Lafferty foam sanitation system, a 3-basin sink, and a scale and label printer.
  - Fit-out includes a meat rail, polytop tables, cooling, waterproof electrical, hot water plumbing and floor drains.
  - Insulated doors at each end connect to the Carcass Aging Cooler and to the Finished Goods Freezer.
- **Carcass Aging Cooler:** "12-foot-wide by 50-foot-long ... built to hold 40,000 lbs—approximately 50 head or 100 sides of beef, assuming 800 lbs per head or 400 per side."
  - "A high 10-foot rail allows you to hang full-length half carcasses, reducing waste when quartering for the aging process."
  - It has a "4-meat rail system" including "a fourth, separate bypass rail for animals that do not require aging".
  - Cooling units "circulate air at a low velocity to ensure proper aging".
  - Capacity grows by adding more Carcass Aging Coolers.
- **Preowned 36 ft Mobile Harvest Unit** (on offer 2026, no price given): a carcass drawdown cooler "sized to chill 3,000 lb of hot, full-length carcasses to below 40 degrees in 24 hours", plus a generator, water tank and pump, and an on-demand water heater.
- **Systems page:** modules "require significantly less power, water" than fixed plants and are "depreciable over 7 years". During design Friesla covers "site responsibilities, preparation, and utilities information—from power and water to waste management". One PS Compact System is a single 58 ft module containing harvest, drawdown and aging rooms (bison, for a tribe).

## Derived sizing [inferred]
- Friesla aging cooler: 600 sq ft for 50 head → **12 sq ft per head**, less than Iowa State's roughly 15-17 sq ft per beef. Rail: 3 aging rails x about 50 ft = 150 ft for 100 sides → **about 1.5 linear ft per side** (sides hung tight; 400 lb sides).
- Friesla drawdown: 3,000 lb in 24 h ≈ 4 head of 800 lb hot carcass per day.
- Fixed aging cooler with a visiting cut unit: holding 14-21 days at N head/week needs 2-3 x N head of rail [inferred].
- None of the three vendors publishes a docking spec for a mobile cut unit at a fixed cooler: shore-power amperage, potable water connection or gray-water discharge. Sentinel's line-power transfer switch and 60 gal RV drain suggest the minimum: a shore-power pedestal, potable hose bib and gray-water dump or holding tank at the cooler site [inferred].
