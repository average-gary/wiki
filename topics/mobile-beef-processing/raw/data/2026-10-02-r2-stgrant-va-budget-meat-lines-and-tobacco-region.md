# Virginia budget meat-processing lines (2022-2028) and the Tobacco Region footprint

## Method
I read the VDACS budget items for "Agricultural Industry Marketing", "Economic Development Services", and "Food Safety and Security" directly from the LIS budget portal:
- 2022-24: 2022 Special Session I, HB30 Chapter 2, Items 98-99: https://budget.lis.virginia.gov/item/2022/2/HB30/Chapter/1/99/
- 2024-26: 2024 Special Session I, HB6001 Chapter 2, Items 86, 87, 92: https://budget.lis.virginia.gov/item/2024/2/HB6001/Chapter/1/87/
- 2025 amendments: HB1600, Chapter 725, Item 87: https://budget.lis.virginia.gov/item/2025/1/HB1600/Chapter/1/87/
- 2026-28: 2026 Special Session I, HB30 Chapter, Items 86-87: https://budget.lis.virginia.gov/item/2026/2/HB30/Chapter/1/87/

I searched each item's text for "meat", "slaughter", "abattoir", "local food", and "beginning farmer". The 2021 sessions and other agencies' items (DHCD, VEDP, VCE/Virginia Tech) were not scanned.

## Findings: AFID appropriations (Item 87/99, "Economic Development Services", authority Title 3.2 Ch. 3.1)
| Budget | FY yr 1 | FY yr 2 | Set-asides |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022-24 (Ch. 2) | $1,500,000 | $1,250,000 | $330,905/yr may pay admin costs; $1M FY23 Dairy Margin Coverage premium assistance |
| 2024-26 (Ch. 2) | $2,250,000 | $1,250,000 | $600k/yr Dairy MC Fund; $250k/yr Blue Catfish Processing, Flash Freezing, and Infrastructure Grant Program (§ 3.2-312) |
| 2025 amend. (Ch. 725) | $2,500,000 | $1,500,000 | Blue Catfish $250k/yr carved from AFID |
| 2026-28 (HB30 Ch.) | $1,500,000 | $1,500,000 | Blue Catfish $250k/yr carved from AFID |

Each version includes this clause: "at the discretion of the Governor, the cap on the amount of funding that may be awarded to an individual project as provided in § 3.2-305 ... may be waived for qualifying projects of regional or statewide interest."

The 2024-26 budget moved the "Virginia Farmland Preservation Fund" and "Agricultural Vitality Program Fund" balances to the Department of Forestry (HB 892, 2024).

## Findings: other VDACS lines
- **Item 92 Food Safety and Security, FY2025 and FY2026:** Regulation of Meat Products was $5,826,821 a year. Paragraph D provides $416,130 a year from the general fund plus $416,130 a year in federal funds, with 8 positions, for "meat and poultry inspection activities". Establishments pay for overtime and holiday inspection. This money funds the state inspection program, not grants.
- **Item 86, 2024-26 and 2026-28:** commodity board pass-throughs. The Cattle Industry Board receives $800,000 a year in checkoff-type excise revenue. The item also funds wine and spirits promotion and $30,000 a year for a VDACS/Virginia State University Small Farm Management Agents partnership "with an emphasis on small, socially disadvantaged, BIPOC, new and beginning, veteran and women farmers". This is outreach, not a grant to businesses.
- **No line was found** that names meat processing, slaughter, abattoirs, or mobile processing units in any of the four budgets checked. [inferred] Virginia's state support for meat processing from 2022 to 2026 flows through the general AFID Fund, including the round-1 AFID Infrastructure grant, rather than a separate appropriation.

## Tobacco Region Revitalization Commission (TRRC)
Source: https://revitalizeva.org/about-the-commission/ and https://revitalizeva.org/programs/
- The service area is "40 tobacco-dependent localities in Southern and Southwest Virginia". Only "local governments and nonprofits serving those localities are eligible to apply."
- **Counties:** Amelia, Appomattox, Bedford, Bland, Buchanan, Brunswick, Buckingham, Campbell, Carroll, Charlotte, Cumberland, Dickenson, Dinwiddie, Floyd, Franklin, Grayson, Greensville, Halifax, Henry, Lee, Lunenburg, Mecklenburg, Nottoway, Patrick, Pittsylvania, Prince Edward, Russell, Scott, Smyth, Sussex, Tazewell, Washington, Wise, Wythe.
- **Cities:** Bristol, Danville, Emporia, Galax, Martinsville, Norton.
- **No Shenandoah Valley locality is included.** That rules out Frederick, Clarke, Warren, Shenandoah, Page, Rockingham, and Augusta counties, the City of Winchester, and Harrisonburg.
- The programs include TRIAD (Tobacco Region Incentive for Agribusiness Development) for localities attracting agriculture and forestry businesses, and the Tobacco Region Opportunity Fund. "All programs require dollar-for-dollar matching funds." The FY27 Southern Virginia Program deadline is Oct 15, 2026.
- [inferred] TRRC money matters to a Frederick County startup in one case only: if the business sited part of its operation in a footprint county such as Bedford or Floyd. For a Valley-based mobile unit, TRRC is not applicable.
