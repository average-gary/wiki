## Stated technical limits (walk-in FAQ and general FAQ)
- **Floor:** "Units with an insulated floor can maintain temperatures as low as 34-36 degrees, while those without a floor can maintain temperatures as low as 38-40 degrees. All outdoor coolers have an insulated floor."
- **Setpoint:** "the minimum recommended temperature is 34°F to avoid freezing up the A/C."
- **No freezing:** "The CoolBot cannot maintain freezing temperatures". For freezers the vendor refers buyers to Arctic Walk-ins.
- **Trailers:** "The unit is not designed or rated for placement on a moving trailer." It needs a stationary floor. That rules it out for the mobile unit, but it is acceptable for a fixed cooler [inferred].
- **Electrical:** "Smaller 6' x 6' units operate on 120V with a 15-amp power supply, whereas larger 12' x 20' units necessitate 220V with a 20-amp power supply."
- **Rail:** "We have a rail system for select sizes of turnkey coolers... only included with complete units and is not sold separately."
- **Sizing example:** "our recommendation for an 8x8x8 room is 12,000 BTU's to be at 38°F or above." Oversizing by one or two A/C sizes (15-18K BTU) gives faster recovery or temperatures below 37°F. 18K and 24K BTU A/Cs run on 220 V.
- **Recovery:** the A/C "typically uses only one 300 watt fan". The FAQ notes "recovery times back to the desired temperature can be slower than a conventional compressor." Energy Saver mode "cools down field-hot produce more slowly".
- **Panels:** 15-year warranty. The CoolBot Pro and LG A/C carry 1-year warranties.

## Aging conditions (vendor blog "An Expert's Guide to Meat Hanging")
- Aging "Must be aged at <40° F" and "Takes less than a month".
- Dry aging: "temperature and humidity need to be tightly controlled. They should fall around 38° F and 75%, respectively." Door openings make conditions "fluctuate rapidly".
- This is a lay source. The Iowa State guide specifies 34°F holding (see the r3 cooler-design Iowa State file).

## Fit for a beef hanging cooler [all inferred]
- **Holding and aging:** a floored CoolBot box at 34-36°F can likely hold already-chilled sides for 7-21 days. This depends on the box keeping 34-36°F at the RH needed for aging. The vendor gives no RH control, and window A/Cs dehumidify.
- **Warm carcass pulldown is the weak point:**
  - The Iowa State pre-chill spec is about 5,000 lb from 100°F to 36°F in 24 h.
  - At about 0.8 BTU/lb·°F, that is roughly 256,000 BTU, or about 10,700 BTU/h before wall, door and evaporative load.
  - Window A/C capacity derates sharply at a 34°F room.
  - So a CoolBot room can likely chill only a few warm sides per day, unless the mobile unit's own drawdown cooler (e.g. Friesla's 3,000 lb/24 h) delivers carcasses already at or below 40°F.
- **Comparison:** a $5,100-plus turnkey CoolBot walk-in is an order of magnitude cheaper than commercial box-and-condensing-unit coolers. It still needs a separate rail and hoist, which must be structurally supported.
