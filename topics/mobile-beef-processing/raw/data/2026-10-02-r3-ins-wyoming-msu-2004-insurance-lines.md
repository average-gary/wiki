# Mobile Slaughter Unit for Wyoming (2004): insurance lines

**Project:** a Federal-State Marketing Improvement Program grant, project report dated July 1, 2004. Collaborators were the Wyoming Business Council, the Wyoming Rural Development Council, the Wyoming Department of Agriculture, and Sheridan College. Principal investigators were Dave Fisher and Jim Bennage (Sheridan College). Contracted contributors included Bruce Dunlop (President, Lopez Island Farm) and Andy Rose (Mid-America Manufacturing Technology Center). The study assessed a **USDA-inspected** mobile unit, because "Currently in Wyoming, there are no USDA inspected plants."

## Observations [inferred]

- **Structure match.** The study splits insurance between the mobile unit ("auto portion") and the fixed cut-and-wrap facility. This is the right accounting split for the Virginia plan: commercial auto plus inland marine on the truck and MSU, and property, GL, and products cover on the shop.
- **No workers' comp line.** Workers' comp sits inside "Payroll tax & benefits", which works out to 15% of wages (for example $6,000 on the $40,000 GM salary, and $2,041 on $13,604). Kill-floor and cutting labor (NCCI 2081) would normally carry a much higher workers' comp rate than the GM's clerical-type exposure. The flat 15% hides that difference.
- **Probably incomplete.** $976 a year for a cut-and-wrap facility is too low to include meaningful general, product, or spoilage liability. The report does not itemize what the line covers. Treat it as a 2004 property-only placeholder.
- **Comparison points for the plan's insurance line, all from NMPAN-linked studies:**
  - Wyoming 2004 MSU plus cut-and-wrap: about $3,842 a year.
  - CISA 2013 two-person cut shop: $9,600 a year.
  - Tennessee USDA plant feasibility study (squarespace-hosted, NMPAN-linked): "Insurance $16,315, 6.1%" [seen in a text grep only, not extracted].
  - Modoc County CA 2024 feasibility study: "Insurance: $60,000 per year (fixed) based on Morrison's experience with similar meat [plants]", rising 3% a year [seen in a text grep only, not extracted].
  - The spread reflects plant scale, the year, and how many coverage lines each budget includes.
