# Nevada MSU + Stationary Fabrication Facility Cost Estimates (2007)

Excerpted with permission on NMPAN from Curtis, K.R., M. Cowee, A. Acosta, W. Hu, S. Lewis, T. Harris. 2007. *Locally Produced Livestock Processing and Marketing Feasibility Assessment*. Technical Report UCED 2006/07-13, University Center for Economic Development, Univ. of Nevada, Reno. NMPAN notes: "None of it was built, but the cost and revenue projections provide a useful template." Design: **two mobile slaughter units plus one permanent fabrication facility** (Silver Springs, NV) under USDA inspection and HACCP. Financial projections are in a companion xls on NMPAN (not extracted). All dollar figures are **2006-2007**.

## Mobile units
- **Labor:** each unit is run by 2 people covering 4 roles. Butchers at about $30,056/yr (2006). Assistants/drivers about $25,376/yr. A plant HACCP coordinator at $46,221/yr (range $39,940-$56,849), "can be combined with the on-site butcher position." Plus 15% for taxes and benefits.
- **Fuel:** service circle about 200 miles; base 150 miles/day; about 5 mpg; diesel $2.82/gal (EIA 2007). The two units cost **$230/day** ($200 fuel + $30 repairs).
- **Offal:** 93% of surveyed producers had on-farm offal disposal, so disposal cost was excluded and waste assumed left on the farm.
- **Corrals:** 68% of producers lacked corrals. Five-foot portable corrals cost $250-$800 (excluded from the analysis).
- **Tractor (truck):** $20,000-$90,000; $45,000 used; depreciation $3,800/yr over 10 years.
- **Start-up for both MSUs: $413,650**, 10-year loan at 9%, **$5,239.94/month**. Other costs: auto insurance, supplies, licensing.

## Fixed fabrication facility (what the MSU still needs)
Described process: the MSU delivers the carcass, it is washed, then chilled in a cooler at -3 C (27 F) for 24 hours, then held at 0 C (32 F) for "a minimum of seven days, although 14-21 days is more common," before cutting. Space is needed for the 24-hour chill, two-week storage, a viscera room, a cutting/processing room and a shipping bay. Constant hot water and power are required.

- "PLEASE NOTE: after this report was published, the authors determined a more accurate price for the processing facility building is $800,000, not $1.3 million."
- Refrigeration: two 10x30 ft coolers plus one freezer, about **$100,000** installed (Bush Refrigeration, 2007), included in the building cost.
- Depreciation $38,611/yr (39-year non-residential). Equipment repair/replacement $100/month.
- **Facility labor:** plant manager $72,100/yr (also schedules the MSUs); brand/marketing manager $83,441; lead butcher $42,000; additional butchers about $11/hr ($21,440/yr); seasonal labor about $15/hr for 24 weeks/yr; office staff $12/hr ($24,788). Plus 15% taxes and benefits.
- **Insurance:** about $71,000/yr total (builder's risk, workers' comp, auto and trailers, general liability, property), estimate only.
- The model is vertically integrated: cost of goods = price paid to the farmer, revenue = retail.

## Takeaways [inferred]
- At 2007 prices the fixed chill/age/cut facility (about $0.9-1.5M) cost roughly 4-7x one MSU (about $207k). Mobility does not remove the main capital cost unless an existing inspected cut-and-wrap partner supplies the cooler space.
- The 7-21 day aging requirement sets the cooler rail-space need: roughly 1-3 weeks of kill volume on the rail.
