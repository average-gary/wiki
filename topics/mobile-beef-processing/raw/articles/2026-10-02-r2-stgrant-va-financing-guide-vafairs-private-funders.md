# Virginia agribusiness financing reference, VAFAIRS, and private/local funders

## 1. VDACS "Quick Reference Guide of Financial Resources for Agriculture & Forestry Businesses in Virginia"
- **Source:** https://www.vdacs.virginia.gov/pdf/financial.pdf, 9 pages, "Updated October 2023". VDACS's Office of Agriculture and Forestry Development page links it as the list of "financing resources for agribusiness" (https://www.vdacs.virginia.gov/marketing-agriculture-and-forestry-development.shtml). The VDACS contact for all three AFID grants is Rachel Meyers, 804-786-6911.
- **Age caveat:** some USDA lines are outdated by 2026. Round-1 sources cover VAPG, MPILP, and MPIRG status.

### VDACS programs
- **AFID Facility Grant:** "not to exceed $500,000 or 25% of qualified capital investment (the lesser)". Requires a dollar-for-dollar local match and at least 30% Virginia-grown inputs. Grants go to political subdivisions.
- **AFID Infrastructure Grant:** up to $50,000, with reduced match "for projects benefiting multiple small-scale agricultural producers". Covered in round 1.
- **AFID Planning Grant:** $20,000 for a single locality or $35,000 for multiple localities.
- **Specialty Crop Block Grant:** up to $75,000 for associations and groups. Livestock is not a specialty crop, so it does not apply [inferred].
- **Organic Cost Share:** 75% of certification cost, up to $750.

### Virginia Small Business Financing Authority (VSBFA), for for-profit small businesses
- **Cash Collateral Program:** "Maximum cash collateral is $500,000 or 40% of the bank loan or line, whichever is less."
- **Loan Guarantee Program:** "guarantees up to 75% of a bank loan", with a maximum guaranty of $750,000.
- **Economic Development Loan Fund:** fills the gap between debt and equity for "qualifying new and expanding businesses, including agri-businesses that are creating new jobs". Covers fixed assets (land, building, equipment). VSBFA's portion is "up to 40% of the total project amount or $1,000,000, whichever is less."
- **Small Business Microloan:** "up to $25,000 to existing small businesses for equipment and working capital". Requires "a minimum credit score of 650 and two years in business". Terms up to 48 months. A day-one startup would not qualify.
- **Contact:** Joe Shearin, 804-371-0008.

### SBA and USDA programs open to for-profits
- **SBA Microloan 7(m):** max $50,000, for equipment and working capital.
- **SBA 7(a):** up to $5M with a 75-85% guaranty.
- **SBA Express:** up to $350k.
- **SBA 504:** 50% senior lender, 40% CDC, and "at least 10 percent equity" from the business.
- **USDA B&I Guaranteed Loan:** rural areas outside towns of 50,000 or more. Requires "20% tangible balance sheet equity for new businesses". Winchester itself is under 50k [inferred, needs verification].
- **USDA RBDG:** grants go to public bodies and nonprofits for "small and emerging private business enterprises" with 50 or fewer employees and $1M or less revenue. "Funds do not go directly to the business." Grants up to $500k, with priority under $100k.
- **USDA IRP:** loans through intermediaries.
- **USDA RCDG:** grants for cooperative development centers.

### Other entries
- **Farm Credit:** Young, Beginning, and Small Producers Program (education and mentoring).
- **Partner Community Capital:** $35k-$500k debt. Focused on Southwest and Southside, so it does not apply to the Valley.
- **Tobacco Commission programs:** Southern and Southwest Virginia only.

## 2. VAFAIRS (Virginia Foundation for Agriculture, Innovation and Rural Sustainability)
- **Sources:** https://vafairs.com/ (about-us, opportunities, faq). The VDACS guide lists it as a "Cooperative Development Center", contact Whitney Perkins, whitney.perkins@vafb.com, an address that indicates a Virginia Farm Bureau affiliation.
- **Services:** feasibility studies, business plans, strategic planning, and grant assistance. It "Specializes in assisting producers in applying for USDA's Value Added Producer Grant." It also offers Cooperative Development Services.
- **Fees:** "Many VAFAIRS services are available at no cost, while some more in-depth services, such as grant writing, business plans, and feasibility studies, may have a small fee."
- **No grants to businesses.** The FAQ says: "There are no programs that currently hand out free money to farmers looking to purchase land, start or expand their operations." It also says: "Very few grant programs help cover the cost of construction or equipment ... Most land, equipment, and construction projects require a loan."
- **Competitions:**
  - Virginia FAIRS Agricultural and Forestry Innovation Challenge, "Bull Pen": "currently paused and expected to return in 2026". The 2023 guide put prizes at $30K.
  - Farm Bureau Ag Innovation Challenge (national, run by AFBF): "awards up to $100,000 to startups creating solutions for farmers' biggest challenges". [inferred] A mobile processing unit might fit if framed as a solution to a farmer processing bottleneck. 2026-27 dates were not verified.
- **Other programs listed:** the Alliance to Advance Climate-Smart Agriculture ($100 per acre or animal unit) and the Virginia State Dairymen's FARM'N project. These are for producers, not processors.

## 3. Virginia Farm Bureau (vafb.com)
- The Young Farmers program offers an Achievement Award and other recognition, plus scholarships. No business grant program was found on the site. VAFAIRS is its business-assistance arm.

## 4. Community Foundation of the Northern Shenandoah Valley (cfnsv.org/grants)
- "All grants and distributions must be made to qualified nonprofit organizations." Opportunities are announced through its Grant Portal. A for-profit startup is ineligible. [inferred] Only a nonprofit partner, such as a producer cooperative or a food-access nonprofit, could seek CFNSV money, and only for a charitable purpose.

## 5. PEC Buy Fresh Buy Local (pecva.org)
- This is a marketing and directory program: "creates food guides and marketing materials, conducts educational outreach, and helps to develop new market outlets." PEC coordinates 3 of Virginia's 9 chapters: Northern Piedmont, the greater Charlottesville area, and Loudoun County.
- No grants were found. Frederick County and Winchester are outside PEC's chapters. [inferred] It is still useful as a free listing and marketing channel for customers in Clarke and Loudoun counties.

## Not reached
- **Farm Aid:** farmaid.org grant pages returned 403/404. From memory, and not verified: its grants go to nonprofit farm organizations, plus small emergency grants to farm families. It would not fund a for-profit processor.
- **Frederick County EDA (yesfrederickva.com, fcva.us) and Winchester (winchesterva.gov):** all returned 403 (bot protection). Local incentive terms are unverified. Contact the EDA directly.
- **Virginia Cooperative Extension beginning-farmer grants:** the ext.vt.edu page returned 404. The 2026-28 budget's only "new and beginning" farmer line is a $30k/yr VDACS-VSU outreach partnership (see the budget file). That is not a grant.
