# Fauquier County considers adding another slaughterhouse (Fauquier Times, 2024-11-22)

> **Sourcing note.** This is a paywalled news article. The text kept here is a paraphrase of the facts it
> reports, with no quotations. Verify them against primary records: the Fauquier Agricultural
> Development office, or the VDACS AFID program, which holds completed planning-grant studies.

## Reported facts (paraphrased)

- **Follow-on grant:** Fauquier's agriculture department and the Fauquier Livestock Exchange (FLX) board in
  Marshall received a **$9,800 AFID planning grant** to keep exploring an abattoir at the exchange. The state
  Agriculture Commissioner presented it as continued state support for the project. This follows the
  Oct 2022 $20k AFID planning grant recorded in round 2 [inferred link].
- **Study outcome:** a county-commissioned study found that about **92% of farmers surveyed** think the county
  needs an additional abattoir. The article links a presentation file, apparently by "NVA Research" and updated
  February 2024, but it sits on a private file share and could not be retrieved.
- **Where the cattle go now:** most Fauquier farmers use Fauquier's Finest in Bealeton. Many travel to plants in
  **Stephens City, Amissville or Middletown**. Stephens City and Middletown are both in or near Frederick County.
- **Backlog:** the county's economic development director described local plants as typically booked about **a
  year** ahead, with farmers booking slots as soon as calves are born.
- **Rationale:** keep processing value inside the county instead of sending it elsewhere with the animals.
- **Status, Nov 2024:** early stage. The county was waiting on **water tests at Marshall**, and a water-table impact
  study might be required.

## Relevance [inferred]

- This is evidence of unmet slaughter demand in the northern Piedmont, next to the target market. Fauquier
  farmers already haul to the Frederick County area.
- For a fixed abattoir, the gating issue was **water**. A mobile unit carrying its own water avoids that problem.
- A backlog of about a year is consistent with VDACS RD894 (18–24 months).
- Unknown: whether the FLX abattoir went ahead after Nov 2024.
