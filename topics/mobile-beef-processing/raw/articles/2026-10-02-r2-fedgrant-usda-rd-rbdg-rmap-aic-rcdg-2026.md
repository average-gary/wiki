# USDA Rural Development intermediary programs FY2026

Sources:
- The rd.usda.gov program pages for RBDG and RMAP, fetched through a 2026 Wayback snapshot because the live site returns 403.
- Grants.gov `fetchOpportunity` records for RDBCP-RBDG-2026 (id 362398), RD-RBCS-26-RMAP (361373), RBCS-2026-AIC (363833) and USDA-NIFA-OP-012058 RCDG (363088).

Key structural point [inferred]: none of these four programs gives money directly to a for-profit start-up processor. A start-up reaches the money in one of two ways:
- as the ultimate borrower or beneficiary of an eligible intermediary;
- by organizing a nonprofit or cooperative that partners with such an intermediary.

## Rural Business Development Grants (RBDG), 7 CFR 4280 Subpart E

- **Purpose.** "Promote economic development and job creation projects". There are two categories, business **Opportunity** grants and business **Enterprise** grants.
- **Who may apply.**
  - rd.usda.gov: "a Public Body/Government Entity, an Indian Tribe, or a Nonprofit entity primarily serving rural areas."
  - Grants.gov FY2026 adds "Rural cooperatives (if organized as a private nonprofit corporation)."
  - Grants.gov also states: "For profit entities, individuals and individual businesses are not eligible to receive grants under this program."
- **Area.** The project must benefit rural areas or towns "outside the urbanized periphery of any city with a population of 50,000 or more." [inferred] Winchester city and its urbanized fringe may be excluded. Outlying Frederick County addresses should be checked with the RD eligibility map.
- **Funding.** "There is no maximum grant amount; however, smaller requests are given higher priority. There is no cost sharing requirement." Opportunity grants are capped at 10% of annual RBDG funding. Enterprise grants "must be used on projects to benefit small and emerging businesses in rural areas."
- **Enterprise uses relevant to a mobile slaughter unit:**
  - feasibility studies and business plans;
  - "plants, machinery, equipment";
  - "producer service improvements";
  - "capitalization of revolving loan funds, including funds that will make loans for start-ups and working capital";
  - rural business incubators.
- **Scoring.**
  - job creation at local businesses;
  - percent of non-federal funding committed;
  - economic need;
  - consistency with local priorities;
  - grantee experience.
- **FY2026 status.** Grants.gov RDBCP-RBDG-2026 was posted May 15, 2026 and closed **June 30, 2026**. The SECD sub-track closed June 15, 2026. Estimated funding was **$27,700,000**, with about 450 awards and no floor or ceiling. Applications go through the RD state office (Virginia RD state office, Richmond). No FY2027 notice had been posted as of 2026-10-02.

## Rural Microentrepreneur Assistance Program (RMAP), 7 CFR 4280 Subpart D

- RMAP lends and grants to **Microenterprise Development Organizations (MDOs)**: nonprofits, tribes and institutions of higher education.
- **Ultimate recipients:** "Businesses located in an eligible area with 10 or fewer full-time employees." The program's stated purpose is to help "microenterprises startup and growth."
- **Terms to ultimate recipients:** "Up to $50,000. Fixed interest rate. Limited to 75 percent of project cost." Uses include working capital, debt refinancing, "Purchasing equipment and supplies" and improving real estate.
- **MDO side:**
  - TA grants of "up to $100,000 annually" with "at least 15 percent matching funds";
  - MDO loans of $50,000-$500,000, with $2.5M aggregate debt, a 20-year term and a 2-year deferral.
- **FY2026 status.** RD-RBCS-26-RMAP (grant) and RD-RBCS-26-RMAP-LOAN were posted Feb 26, 2026 and closed **June 30, 2026** ($3.2M estimated, about 20 grant awards). rd.usda.gov also says "Applications are accepted on an ongoing basis" through state offices.
- [inferred] What matters to a start-up is whether a Virginia MDO currently holds RMAP funds. The borrower applies to that MDO, not to USDA.

## Agriculture Innovation Center (AIC) Grant Program, FY2026: OPEN

- RBCS-2026-AIC was posted **Sep 9, 2026** and closes **Jan 31, 2027**. It offers $2.5M estimated, 3 awards of **$600,000-$1,000,000**, with cost sharing required.
- **Objective:** "grants to Agriculture Innovation Centers that will provide technical assistance to agricultural producers to market value-added agricultural products."
- [inferred] This program is relevant if a Virginia university, nonprofit or the producer group wanted to become a center that serves meat-processing producers. It does not fund the processor itself.

## Rural Cooperative Development Grants (RCDG): now NIFA-administered

- USDA-NIFA-OP-012058 was posted Jul 7, 2026 and closed **Jul 24, 2026** ($5M, 12 awards of $200K-$1M, cost share required). The FY2025 round, USDA-NIFA-OP-011616, also ran under NIFA (Sep 4-15, 2025).
- Applicants are nonprofit institutions only (7 CFR 4284.520). The grants fund centers "that in turn provide technical assistance to start, expand, or improve cooperatives."
- [inferred] A producer cooperative would get free help from a funded Center. In the Mid-Atlantic, which center that is should be confirmed.
