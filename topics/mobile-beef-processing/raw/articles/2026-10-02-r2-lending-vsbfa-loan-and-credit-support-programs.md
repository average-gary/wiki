# VSBFA Loan and Credit Support Programs (2026)

Pages fetched 2026-10-02:
- sbsd.virginia.gov/loan-programs/
- sbsd.virginia.gov/credit-support-programs/
- Capital Connect fact sheet (dated 8/22/2024)
- Cash Collateral fact sheet (dated 8/22/2024)

The old vsbfa.virginia.gov domain no longer resolves; VSBFA content now lives under sbsd.virginia.gov.

VSBFA contact: 600 East Broad Street, Suite 300, Richmond, VA 23219. Phone (804) 786-6585. Email VSBFA@sbsd.virginia.gov.

## Direct loan programs

### Microloan Program

"a direct loan from the VSBFA to the business client that does not require a bank's participation."

**Eligibility:** the business must:
- be "currently operating a business in Virginia"
- be in good standing with the SCC
- have a "credit score of 650 or higher"
- meet one size test: revenue of $10M or less in each of the last three years, net worth of $2M or less, or fewer than 250 employees

**Uses:** business acquisition, "Equipment and fixed asset purchases", and working capital (some restrictions).

**Not allowed:** construction, non-owner-occupied real estate, or refinancing.

**Terms:**
- "Loan amounts up to $150,000 for qualifying applicants"
- 5–7 year repayment
- automatic monthly debit
- $150 non-refundable application fee
- the application form is dated 2026-02-25

### Economic Development Loan

"a companion loan for fixed-asset financing needs designed to fill the financing gap between the bank's loan and private equity." The landing page gave no further terms; the application PDF dates from 2019.

### Capital Connect Program (SSBCI 2.0), per the fact sheet dated 8/22/2024

- **Structure:** "Direct loans originated by VSBFA in conjunction with separate financing being concurrently provided by a private-sector lender."
- **Size:** "up to $1,000,000 or a maximum of 40% of the total project costs, whichever is less."
- **Eligibility:** under 250 employees in Virginia and under 750 nationwide.
- **Program emphasis:** SSBCI favours "very small businesses (VSBs) with fewer than 10 employees" and businesses owned by socially and economically disadvantaged individuals (SEDIs).
- **Terms:**
  - "Amortization up to 25 years with a loan term < 10 years"
  - fixed rate set at closing
  - "Personal guarantees required of any principal with more than 20% ownership"
  - no confession-of-judgment language allowed
- **Fees:** all program fees, including the $500 application fee, are "temporarily" waived because federal SSBCI funding is time-limited. The borrower still pays closing legal fees.
- **Eligible uses:** fixed assets and real estate (restrictions apply).
- **Ineligible uses:** goodwill, passive or residential real estate, and stock purchases.
- **Turnaround:** a credit decision "typically ... within a week" of a complete application.

## Credit support programs (for lenders)

### Classic Loan Guaranty Program

"a traditional deficiency guaranty of a bank loan. It can be used for lines of credit or term loans any time the bank believes it needs additional support."

### Cash Collateral Program (SSBCI 2.0), per the fact sheet dated 8/22/2024

- **Support:** cash collateral of "up to 40% of the bank's loan amount or a maximum of $1,000,000, whichever is less". Loans over $20M are ineligible.
- **Eligible uses:**
  - "Working Capital Lines of Credit, Term Loans to Finance Equipment and Other Fixed Asset Purchases"
  - owner-occupied real estate
- **Where the deposit sits:** at a Virginia bank eligible to hold public deposits (§ 2.2-4409).
- **Fees:** the $200 application fee and the support fee are temporarily waived.

### Loan to CDFIs (SSBCI 2.0)

This lets eligible CDFIs "expand their lending to eligible small businesses and non-profits with funding from SSBCI 2.0 and VSBFA." Contacts: Anna Mackley and Cheryl Bostick.

## Relevance to a Frederick County MSU [inferred]

- **Microloan:** the "currently operating" requirement probably rules out a pre-revenue startup. If the operator is already in business, for example doing custom cutting or running a farm LLC, $150k over 5–7 years would cover a used trailer or part of a new one.
- **Typical startup stack:**
  1. A bank or Farm Credit term loan secured by the trailer.
  2. VSBFA Cash Collateral of up to 40% of the loan to cover the specialized-collateral discount lenders apply to a custom MSU.
  3. Alternatively, Capital Connect providing up to 40% of project cost as a subordinate piece.
- **Timing risk:** SSBCI 2.0 money runs out over time. The fee waivers say so explicitly.
