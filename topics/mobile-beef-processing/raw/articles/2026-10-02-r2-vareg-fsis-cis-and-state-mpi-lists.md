## 1. Cooperative Interstate Shipment (CIS) program

Quote: "Under CIS, state-inspected plants can operate as federally-inspected facilities, under specific conditions, and ship their product in interstate commerce and may have the opportunity to export them to foreign countries, provided the CIS participating State has entered into a supplemental agreement that addresses the export of CIS inspected products. No states currently have a supplemental agreement for exporting product."

"The CIS program is limited to plants located in the 29 states that have established a Meat and Poultry Inspection Program (MPI) and maintain 'at least equal to' FSIS regulatory standards."

**Currently participating states, with the date each agreement was signed:**

**Virginia is not listed.** West Virginia is not listed either. As of this snapshot, a Virginia state-inspected plant cannot ship across state lines under CIS. [inferred] A mobile unit in Frederick County that wants to sell into WV/MD/DC needs a federal (USDA FSIS) grant of inspection, not a Virginia state grant.

## 2. State-Federal Cooperative Inspection Program (SFCIP, the "at least equal to" State MPI program)

- "FSIS allocates approximately $50 million annually to support these State inspection programs, providing up to 50 percent of the operating funds."
- "Product inspected under an 'at least equal to' State MPI program can only be sold within that State (intrastate commerce). Establishments may choose whether to apply for Federal or State inspection in States that operate an 'at least equal to' State MPI program."
- Legislative authority: 21 U.S.C. 454 and 661.
- **Virginia's entry:** Office of Meat & Poultry Services, VDACS, Oliver Hill Building, 102 Governor Street, Suite 352, Richmond, VA 23219. Gary Milton, Program Manager, (804) 786-4569. Matthew Koranek, Program Analyst, (540) 319-8278. Commissioner Joseph Guthrie. The most recent FSIS audit final report listed for Virginia is FY2024.

## 3. States with and without MPI programs (Dec 2025)

The page lists "30 states currently operating meat and poultry inspection (MPI) programs." This conflicts with the "29" figure on the CIS page, which is older. Virginia is listed as **Meat & Poultry**. Neighboring states:
- West Virginia: Meat & Poultry (state program)
- North Carolina: Meat & Poultry (state program)
- Maryland: gave up both programs on 1-Apr-91 (federal only)
- Pennsylvania: gave up meat on 17-Jul-72 and poultry on 31-Oct-71 (federal only)
- Kentucky and Tennessee: federal only

"All plants under Federal inspection are eligible to sell in interstate commerce."
