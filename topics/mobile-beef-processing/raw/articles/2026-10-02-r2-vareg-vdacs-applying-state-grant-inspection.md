# Applying For a Grant of Inspection with the Office of Meat & Poultry Services (VDACS OMPS)

The PDF is 9 pages, authored by VDACS, with a creation date of Jan 8, 2026.

## General

"State inspected products cannot cross state lines therefore cannot be sold to businesses or people located outside of Virginia. If you are planning on selling products outside of Virginia, you will need to apply with USDA, Food Safety & Inspection Service."

## Facility requirements

- OMPS "does not prescribe the type of materials used". The building only has to allow inspection and stay sanitary. The governing rules are **9 CFR 416.1-416.5**. Recommended references are 62 FR 45045 (1997) and the FSIS Sanitation Performance Standards Compliance Guide (March 2016).
- **Separation:** an official establishment "shall be separate and distinct from any unofficial establishment (e.g., FDA regulated product, custom or retail exempt product). Separation may be accomplished by time or space." [inferred] A single unit could therefore run custom-exempt work and inspected work at different times. Inspection will not be granted in a building with living quarters unless the inspected area is sealed off by solid floors, walls and ceilings.
- **Inspector office:** plants that need one or more full-time inspectors must provide rent-free office space. The Inspection Manager may waive this for plants without a full-time inspector, but those plants must still provide "a lockable storage cabinet" and a workspace.
- **Sewage:** must go to a separate system or otherwise be kept from backing up into product areas. If the system is private, the plant must provide a State or local health authority approval letter on request.
- **Water:** the plant needs a potability letter from the local or state health agency stating the source, its approval, and that it meets EPA Drinking Water Standards. A current water lab report must also be on file before inspection is granted. If the water comes from private wells, the letter must state the wells are on the premises and protected from pollution.

## Food safety systems

- HACCP under **9 CFR 417**. At least one HACCP-trained individual, either staff or a contractor, must develop and maintain the plan. FSIS generic HACCP models are recommended.
- Sanitation SOPs under **9 CFR 416.12-416.16**.
- A written recall plan under **9 CFR 418.3**.
- **Slaughter category:** humane handling under the Humane Methods of Slaughter Act and **9 CFR 313**. Beef plants must meet **SRM removal (9 CFR 310.22)** and address STEC. Plants must consider sanitary dressing. Livestock slaughter facility requirements are in **9 CFR 307.2 and Part 310**.
- Post-lethality-exposed ready-to-eat product requires a Listeria program under 9 CFR 430.

## Hours and inspection charges

- "Official establishments shall be provided five consecutive 8-hour days (per shift) of free inspection service during the basic work week of Sunday through Saturday," not counting lunch.
- The plant submits a work schedule for Inspection Manager approval. Adding or dropping a shift requires **2 weeks' notice**.
- Work beyond 8 hours a day, beyond 40 hours a week, or on holidays is reimbursable "at tenth of an hour increment". A call-back after the inspector has left is billed at a **minimum of 2 hours**. Bills are delinquent after 30 days, and "Overtime or holiday inspection will not be performed for any establishment having a delinquent account."
- Inspector limits: on-line slaughter post-mortem duty is capped at 10 hours a day, and off-line or processing duty at 12 hours a day. Lunch is 30-60 minutes and starts between the 4th and 5th hour.

## Labels and marks

- An official plant number is reserved once OMPS receives the application. Carcasses are ink-branded with the "V.A. Inspection legend", which includes the plant number. Packaged product carries the legend and number on its label.
- Labels must be submitted for approval in three cases: religious-exemption product, special statements and claims (including animal-raising claims such as grass-fed or organic), and temporary-use labels. Generically approved labels under 9 CFR 412.2 do not need submission, but the plant must keep copies, formulations and procedures on file. Submissions go to OMPS@vdacs.virginia.gov.

## Inauguration, conditional grant, refusal

- When the applicant is ready (written SSOP, hazard analysis and HACCP plan, labels, facility), the Regional Supervisor and Inspection Manager schedule an onsite review. If the plant passes, OMPS issues a **"Conditional Grant of Inspection"**. During the conditional period, which may not exceed 90 days, the plant can produce for commerce while it validates the HACCP plan under 9 CFR 417.4.
- The Program Manager may refuse a grant for: no written HACCP plan, no written SSOP, inadequate sanitation, failure to show humane handling, or unfitness under FMIA §401 or PPIA §18(a). A refused applicant may request a hearing under 9 CFR 500.7(b).

## Application

- The application form is **VDACS-03090, "Application for State Meat and Poultry Inspection"**, provided by a program employee. Every block must be completed, with "NA" or "None" where something does not apply. Mail it to VDACS/OMPS, P.O. Box 1163, Richmond, VA 23218. To request the form, call 804.786.4569.
- A **plot plan** of the entire premises is required: buildings, roads, streams, adjoining buildings, surfacing, and a north arrow. It "can be hand drawn", and it must identify the part of the facility to be granted inspection.
- The guide's resource list includes NMPAN and the **"Mobile Slaughter Compliance Guide"**. The guide has no MSU-specific text of its own.
