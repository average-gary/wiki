# 2VAC5-210: Virginia meat and poultry inspection regulations

**Chapter number:** The brief guessed 2VAC5-60, but that chapter is "Rules and Regulations Governing the Operation of Livestock Markets [Repealed]". The meat and poultry chapter is **2VAC5-210**. Statutory authority is Va. Code § 3.2-5406. The Act itself is Va. Code § 3.2-5400 et seq. The chapter has two parts.

## Part I: Adoption by Reference

**2VAC5-210-10. Adoption by reference.** This section adopts USDA's rules "as contained in Title 9, Chapter III, Subchapters A and E of the Code of Federal Regulations, as it exists and has been published in the January 1, 2021, update", "with administrative changes therein as needed to make them appropriate and applicable to intrastate operations". The section was last amended in Virginia Register Vol. 38, Issue 3, effective September 27, 2021. [inferred] Federal amendments made after Jan 1, 2021 do not automatically apply in Virginia until the Board updates the date.

**2VAC5-210-20. Definitions.** These definitions turn the federal text into state text:
- "Federal" means "Virginia."
- "Federally inspected and passed" means Virginia inspected and passed.
- "Interstate" means intrastate.
- "United States" or "U.S." means "Virginia"; "U.S. Brands and Legends" means "Virginia Brands and Legends."
- "Food Safety Inspection Service" means the Virginia meat and poultry inspection program.
- "Program" means the Office of Meat and Poultry Services, VDACS.
- "Administrator" means the Director of the Division of Animal and Food Industry Services.
- "Commerce" means commerce within the Commonwealth.

**2VAC5-210-30. Mandatory inspection and voluntary inspection and certification.** This section adopts 9 CFR Parts 302, 303, 304, 305, 306, 307, 309-320, 325, 329, 335, 350, 352, 354, 355, 362 and 381 (most subparts), with export and import references deleted. It adds two Virginia-specific provisions:

- **Part 303 (Exemptions), custom slaughter:** "Any establishment, firm, person or corporation operating under Section 303.1(a)(2) of this subchapter is required to apply for and receive a permit of exemption in accordance with requirements set forth by the Commissioner of Agriculture and Consumer Services or his delegate." 9 CFR 303.1(a)(2) is the custom slaughter and processing exemption, which covers animals delivered by the owner for that owner's household, nonpaying guests and employees.
- **Part 381 Subpart C, poultry:** anyone operating under § 381.10(5), (6) or (7) must also apply for and receive a permit of exemption.

The regulation does not describe what the permit application involves. It leaves that to "requirements set forth by the Commissioner", meaning VDACS guidance rather than the regulation itself (see the round-1 VDACS custom-exemption guide).

Part 304 (application for inspection; grant or refusal) and Part 307 (facilities for inspection) are adopted as written. A Virginia state grant therefore follows the same application, facility and sanitation framework as a federal grant.

**2VAC5-210-41. Regulatory requirements (Subchapter E).** This section adopts Part 412 (label approval, effective Jan 6, 2014), Part 416 (sanitation and SSOPs), Part 417 (HACCP), Part 418 (recalls), Part 424, Part 430, Part 441, Part 442 and Part 500 (rules of practice).

**Mobile units:** 2VAC5-210 has **no section on mobile slaughter units**. [inferred] A Virginia MSU is regulated through the adopted federal parts, mainly 304, 307 and 416, the same way FSIS treats MSUs federally. For custom-exempt mobile work, the operator also needs the VDACS permit of exemption.

## Part II: Fees and Charges

- **2VAC5-210-60, Hourly charge:** VDACS "may charge for voluntary, overtime, and holiday inspection, and administrative costs associated therewith". The charge covers the salaries and benefits of the inspection personnel who provide those services, plus the attributable share of administrative staff. [inferred] Inspection during regular hours under a mandatory state grant is not billed, which matches the federal model.
- **2VAC5-210-70, Mileage rate:** capped at the rate authorized by the Commonwealth's State Travel Regulations.
- **2VAC5-210-80, Lodging rates:** capped at the State Travel Regulations rate.

The chapter's "FORMS (2VAC5-210)" page lists no forms.
