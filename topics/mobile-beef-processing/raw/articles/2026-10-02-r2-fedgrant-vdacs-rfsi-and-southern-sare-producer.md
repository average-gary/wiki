# VDACS RFSI and Southern SARE Producer Grants

## Part 1: Resilient Food Systems Infrastructure (RFSI), Virginia round (VDACS)

**Sources:**
- The VDACS "Resilient Food Systems and Infrastructure Information Session", March 11, 2024 (https://www.vdacs.virginia.gov/pdf/rfsi-vdacs-webinar.pdf).
- The AMS RFSI page (https://www.ams.usda.gov/services/grants/rfsi, fetched 2026-10-02).
- The old VDACS page `marketing-rfsi.shtml` now returns 404, and Wayback has no snapshot.

**Virginia round terms** (2024 webinar):
- **Dates.** "Request for Applications (RFA) Opens: March 15th 2024. Grant Deadline: April 15, 2024." Proposals were emailed to VDACS.
- **Funding.** "Approximately $6.5 million will be available for competitive subaward grants." Two grant types, and an applicant may choose only one:
  - Infrastructure Grants: minimum $100,000, up to $3 million.
  - Simplified Equipment-Only Grants: minimum $10,000, maximum $100,000.
- **Infrastructure match.** "50% of the total proposed project costs," cash or in-kind.
  - The match falls to 25% for historically underserved producers and SBA small disadvantaged, women-owned or veteran-owned small businesses.
  - For nonprofits and formal cooperatives, "the majority (51% or higher) of the board of directors or owners/operators need to meet the criteria."
  - Other federal funds and program income cannot count as match.
- **Eligible entities** include "Agricultural producers or processors, or groups of agricultural producers and processors" (the list was truncated in the extraction).
- **Priorities.**
  - Federal: underserved, new and beginning, and veteran producers.
  - Virginia: cold storage equipment, expanding processing, aggregation and distribution capacity, and construction of new facilities.
- **Exclusion.** "The primary goal of RFSI is to support food system crops and products meant for human consumption (excluding meat and poultry products, which are funded through other USDA programs)."

**AMS status.** "RFSI has provided about $400 million in funding to all States and Territories." The program-scope document is dated "updated November 2023". AMS directs applicants to state department of agriculture pages. No FY2026-27 RFSI funding notice or new Virginia RFA was found.

[inferred] RFSI is a dead end for a beef slaughter unit on two counts: the meat exclusion, and no open round. Cold storage for non-meat products would not help this business.

## Part 2: Southern SARE 2027 Producer Grant call

Source: Southern SARE, "2027 Producer Grant Call for Proposals", released September 2026 and linked from https://southern.sare.org/grants/.

- **Region.** Virginia is listed in the Southern SARE region.
- **Award.** "Individual Farmers, $20,000; Farmer Groups, $25,000". The project runs two years.
- **Schedule.**
  - September 2026: call released.
  - **Proposals due November 06, 2026 at 12 p.m. (NOON) EST**, through the SARE Grant Management System. Late proposals are not accepted.
  - February 2027: awards announced.
- **Payment.** "Producer Grant funds are paid by reimbursement of allowable project expenses ... Advance payments are not possible."
- **Eligibility.**
  - The applicant must be "a full time or part-time commercial farm business owner, or be part of a farmer organization, such as a cooperative." Farmer organizations need "majority farmer representation on their governing boards."
  - "There is no restriction on farm size or the length of time an individual has been farming."
  - The farm needs "at least $1,000 in documented annual income from the operation."
  - One proposal per farm per year. At least one cooperator is required.
- **Nature of the grant.** "Producer Grants are research grants. Grant funds cannot be used to pay a farmer to farm, fund operating expenses, or conduct any kind of farm business."
- **Non-allowable costs.**
  - "Starting a farm, NGO, business or other community organization, or expanding an existing farm, NGO, business";
  - "Providing support of any kind for capital investments or permanent farm improvements, including: purchasing equipment ... purchasing livestock";
  - "Generally, any item that has permanent use beyond the life of the grant project is not allowed."
- **Allowable costs.**
  - sampling and lab analysis;
  - equipment *rental* within the project timetable;
  - project materials;
  - travel;
  - hired labor;
  - the farmer's own labor beyond normal duties;
  - outreach (field days, workshops, fact sheets, videos).
- **Topic fit.** Listed topics include "Sustainable Agriculture Marketing Projects" and "Expanding and developing domestic markets."

[inferred] **Fit.** A Frederick County cattle producer, or a producer group with a majority-farmer board, could propose a $20K-$25K on-farm study. Example subjects:
- on-farm harvest versus hauling to a plant, measuring shrink, carcass yield, bruising and pH;
- cost per head;
- consumer acceptance of custom-exempt quarter or half sales.

The study would rent an existing mobile unit, such as Blue Ridge Meats (round 1), and run a field day. It cannot buy or build the unit or fund the business launch. The deadline of Nov 6, 2026 is about 5 weeks from the fetch date.
