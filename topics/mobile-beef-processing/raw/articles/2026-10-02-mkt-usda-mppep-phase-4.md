# MPPEP Phase 4 (USDA Rural Development)

Fetched from the Wayback Machine snapshot (web.archive.org/web/2026id_/...) because rd.usda.gov returns 403 to scripted clients. The page says "Application Window: Closed", so the snapshot was taken after August 7, 2026.

## Status and dates
- "Applications will be accepted from May 7, 2026, through August 7, 2026. Applications must be received by 11:59 pm Eastern Time on August 7, 2026, through Grants.gov."
- USDA held a Phase 4 webinar on June 16, 2026.
- Separately, RBCS will reconsider FY2022 Phase I awardees who were denied reimbursement only because they failed to give advance notice of an ownership change. These requests can be submitted until **October 15, 2026**, by email to mppep@usda.gov.

## Purpose
- "The primary purpose of MPPEP Phase 4 is to support the American Beef Industry by diversifying the meat and poultry supply chain and opening local market opportunities. The applicant's facility must primary process cattle in order for the project to be eligible for the program, however, funds or equipment may be used for processing meat and poultry at the facility."

## Eligible applicants
- For-profit organizations, nonprofit organizations, producer-owned cooperatives, Tribes, and Tribal entities.
- The applicant must meet the definition of Very Small, Small, or Intermediate Processor in NOFO Section 1.3.
- It must be "currently engaged in the primary processing of cattle for commercial markets or toll process and been in operation for at least one year (as of the date of the application)."
- It must "currently operate under an FSIS grant of inspection, grant of inspection under a Cooperative Interstate Shipment Program, or a state meat and poultry inspection program with standards at least equal to Federal inspection."
- It must be independently owned and operated, and domestically owned.

## Award structure
- Available funding is "divided equally into two separate competitions: one for Very Small Processors and Small Processors, and one for Intermediate Processors." Each competition is ranked separately.
- **Processing Expansion Projects:** $50,000 to $2 million. These cover equipment-only purchases over $250,000 plus the facility improvements, upgrades, renovations, or retrofits needed to install the equipment. **50% match.**
- **Simplified Equipment-Only Projects:** $10,000 to $250,000. These cover equipment cost only, with no renovation, labor, installation, or certification costs. **25% match.**
- Applicants must verify their cost share before final award approval.

## Technical assistance
- The AMS Meat and Poultry Processing Capacity Technical Assistance Program (MPPTA) offers applicants free help from pre-application through post-award management.

## Relevance to a mobile beef unit [inferred]
- A startup mobile slaughter unit cannot use MPPEP before its first year of inspected cattle processing. It could apply in a later round (if one is held) for equipment such as a second trailer, cooler, or fabrication equipment, through the 25%-match Simplified Equipment-Only track.
- It is unclear whether a mobile unit counts as a "Facility" for the renovation and retrofit items. The page does not address mobile units.
- The page does not say whether a Phase 5 is planned.
