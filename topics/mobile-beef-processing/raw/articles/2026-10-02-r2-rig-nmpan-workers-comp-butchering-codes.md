# NMPAN: Workers' compensation for meat processors

The page was posted on the NMPAN site 2019-07-18 (migrated from the older site). The NCCI scope text is dated "as of December 2008" and the example rates are "effective in Iowa during the 2009 calendar year". It is informational only: "does not constitute legal advice".

## Example NCCI rates (Iowa, 2009; $ per $100 of payroll)

| Code | Title | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| 2081 | Butchering | $10.89 |
| 2089 | Packing House - All Operations | $3.37 |
| 2095 | Meat Products Manufacturing | $3.86 |
| 8021 | Meat, Fish or Poultry Dealer - Wholesale | $2.75 |
| 8031 | Meat, Fish or Poultry - Retail | $5.73 |

NMPAN: "These are example rates. Your rates may be higher or lower, but should be higher or lower in similar proportions, i.e. 'Butchering' should always be far more expensive than the other codes." "In general, most small 'locker' type meat processors should be eligible for code 2081 or 2089 (THIS IS NOT A GUARANTEE)."

## Scope of code 2081 Butchering (NCCI, Dec 2008)
- "Code 2081 covers insureds engaged in the butchering of livestock and the preparation of fresh meat. The class contemplates the handling of livestock including pen maintenance and killing room operations. The dressing, boning and chilling of beef, pork, sheep or calves for sale either in halves, quarters or individual fresh cuts are also contemplated". Rendering, washing casings, salting hides, cooking offal and incidental sausage making are also included.
- Code 2089 applies when "the risk's principal operations must be that of processing meat", such as smoking or curing hams, making bacon, pickling, and often packaging product in consumer form. Butchering plus steaks, chops, roasts and incidental grinding stays in 2081.
- 2081 and 2089 "shall not be assigned to the same risk unless the operations ... are conducted as separate and distinct businesses". Employees are usually split across codes only "when there is physical separation between work areas".
- State variations: 2081 is not used in Oregon (2089 applies there). In Illinois, Missouri and South Dakota, secondary cutting is classified separately under 8021/8031. The Montana and New Jersey definitions include packing house operations. Virginia is not listed among the variations.

## Disputes
NCCI offers (1) a free phone dispute review and (2) an independent inspection at "$115/ hr. for the inspection and most inspections take 3-5 hours" (cost as of the original posting). NCCI Customer Service: (800) 622-4123.

## Implications for a custom-exempt mobile kill business [inferred]
- A kill-and-dress crew working on farms (handling livestock, killing, skinning, quartering) fits the 2081 description. It does not meet the 2089 "principal operations ... processing meat" test.
- At the 2009 Iowa ratio, workers' comp on a two-person crew with $80,000 payroll would be about $8,700/yr under 2081, against about $2,700 under 2089. Get a 2026 Virginia quote. Virginia is an NCCI state [inferred, verify].
- The page does not cover general liability, commercial auto (tow vehicle plus trailer), inland marine/equipment coverage for the rig, or care-custody-control coverage for customers' carcasses. Those premiums are still unquantified for this plan.
