# Frederick County, VA Zoning (Ch. 165): RA district, definitions, slaughterhouse regulations

**Access note:** ecode360.com and fcva.us both return Cloudflare/Akamai 403 to direct fetches. The text was read from Wayback snapshots dated 2026-07-11:
- Part 401 RA: ecode360.com/8708347
- Part 101 definitions: ecode360.com/8707729
- Part 204 specific-use regulations: ecode360.com/13804289

The code lists its most recent zoning amendment as 2026-05-13. The eCode customer ID is FR1364.

## § 165-401.01 Purpose
The RA regulations aim "to preserve large, open parcels of land, tree cover, scenic views, sensitive environmental areas and prime agricultural and locally significant soils".

## § 165-401.02 Permitted (by-right) uses (last amended 4-9-2025)
The by-right uses relevant here are:
- A. "Agriculture, farming, dairies, animal husbandry, and forestry."
- W. "Accessory uses."
- X-Z. Poultry, fish and hog farming (hog pens at least 200 ft from residences).
- JJ. On-premises roadside stand accessory to a bona fide farm.
- NN. Taxidermists.

No meat processing, cold storage, warehouse or locker-plant use appears in the by-right list.

## § 165-401.03 Conditional uses (CUP required; last amended 4-9-2025)
- **"BB. Slaughterhouses."**
- Other CUP uses that show the county's rural-business posture: Q. veterinary/livestock services; EE. agricultural supply cooperatives; O. cottage occupations; G. auction houses; Y. ice cream parlor or bakery.
- E. Restaurants must have "an approved drainfield; alternative waste systems, including pump and hauls, are prohibited". This is a sign of how the county treats wastewater for commercial food uses [inferred].

## § 165-101.02 Definitions (selected)
- **AGRICULTURE and FARMING:**
  - "A. Cultivating the soil or raising or harvesting any agricultural or horticultural commodity on a farm, including the raising ... and management of animals."
  - "B. Handling, drying, packing, grading or storing on a farm any agricultural or horticultural commodity in its unmanufactured state but only if the owner, tenant or operator of the farm regularly produces more than 1/2 of the commodity so treated."
  - C. Forestry.
- **ACCESSORY OR SECONDARY USE:** "A use of land or of a building ... customarily associated with and incidental and subordinate to the principal use ... and located on the same lot with such principal use. A secondary use shall be a use not already permitted by right in a zoning district but may be permitted in conjunction with a permitted use."
- **SLAUGHTERHOUSES:** "Establishments primarily engaged in the slaughtering or processing of meats for human consumption or other related products. The word 'slaughterhouse,' as used in this definition, shall not be construed to prohibit persons who are actually farmers from killing their own cattle, sheep, swine, goats and fowl for their own family use."
- **HOME OCCUPATION:** "In the RA (Rural Areas) Zoning District, a home occupation may be carried on in an accessory building or structure." Under 165-204.37, a home occupation may produce no offensive odor and no more than 5 non-resident people per day, with one business vehicle. A cottage occupation (CUP, 165-204.38) allows up to 10 people per day and commercial vehicles.
- The definitions include "WAREHOUSING, MEDICAL AND ALLIED HEALTH" but no general cold-storage or locker-plant definition was found.

## § 165-204.17 Slaughterhouses and rendering plants (added 12-9-1992; D-G added 9-13-2017)
- "A. It shall be unlawful to operate any slaughterhouse, abattoir, rendering plant or establishment where animals or fowl, dead or alive, are processed or where food or feed is manufactured or processed, unless such place or establishment is maintained and operated in a clean and sanitary manner at all times."
- "B. ... constructed and maintained as to effectively control the entrance of insects and rodents. The doors, windows and other openings thereof shall be fitted with screen doors and wire window screens of not coarser than fourteen-gauge mesh."
- C. Farmer own-use exception (same as the definition).
- "D. All buildings, animal unloading/staging areas, and animal pens shall be a minimum of 100 feet from all property lines."
- "E. Total building(s) square footage should not exceed 20,000 square feet (SF)."
- "F. All operations must be under roof and screened from view from adjoining properties and public streets."
- "G. Additional buffering and screening may be required as specified by the Zoning Administrator."

## Other relevant sections
- **§ 165-204.14 Sewage treatment facilities:** facilities serving 3 or more uses must be dedicated to a public authority, but "Sewage treatment facilities serving primary or accessory agricultural uses shall be exempt". There is a 600 ft buffer to residences and RP zoning.

## Application to a cooler-only site with a docking mobile unit [inferred; needs a Zoning Administrator determination]
1. **By-right agriculture/accessory:** this fits only if the cooler stores the farm's own beef (definition B requires the operator to produce more than half of what is stored, "in its unmanufactured state"). A service cooler holding many owners' carcasses fails the more-than-half test. A slaughtered and dressed carcass is also arguably no longer "unmanufactured".
2. **Slaughterhouse CUP:** the definition covers "processing of meats". 165-204.17A reaches any "establishment where animals ... dead ... are processed". If the mobile unit docks to cut and wrap on the parcel, the site is very likely a processing establishment that needs CUP BB. Expect the 100 ft property-line setbacks, the 20,000 sq ft cap, all operations under roof (a dock canopy or enclosed bay for the trailer), and screening.
3. **Warehouse/cold storage:** this use is not listed for RA at all. It is a commercial or industrial (B2/B3/M1/M2) path, so it would require a rezoning off agricultural land.
4. **Practical route:** request a written use determination under § 165-101.06 (administration and interpretation) before buying equipment. Budget for the CUP process (Planning Commission and Board of Supervisors public hearings) plus a site plan.
