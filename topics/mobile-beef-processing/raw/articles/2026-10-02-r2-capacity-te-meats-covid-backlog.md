# T&E Meats (Harrisonburg): COVID-19 demand, booking backlog, and seasonal lead times

Three pages on temeats.com, all fetched 2026-10-02. The blog post is the main source. The other two add context.

## 1. Blog post "COVID-19 Unleashes Extraordinary Demand for Local Meat Processing" (2020-11-09)

Written in the first person by T&E management. The page byline "Estland" is the web design firm, so the named author is unknown.

- After mid-March 2020, "the phone starting ringing at T&E, and literally did not stop ringing for months." Farmers first cancelled dates as their restaurant accounts collapsed, then "within a few days, they were calling back to book in double what they had just cancelled."
- Example: one farm that normally harvested "2 to 3 beef a month," mostly sold to restaurants, "suddenly sold 92 quarters as locker beef in that initial crazy 6 weeks" (23 whole beeves).
- "Demand was so overwhelming that in late April T&E literally stopped scheduling slaughter for the entire month of May, so that we could save our sanity and catch up."
- "**By late September, T&E was essentially booked up through the end of 2021—16 months out.** It could have been further, but I refuse to schedule into 2022 this far out."
- "Checking with small processors I know around the country, this is practically a universal story."
- "T&E's current list of unscheduled beef and hogs is about two pages long."
- Capital spent to add throughput: "a new $40,000 stuffer and a new $12,000 vacuum sealer, hired more people, re-organized production layouts. But we can only squeeze so much more production from this old building."
- On what is needed: "we are going to have to find a way to rebuild old locker plants and build some new small plants. But that is an expensive proposition. It will call for leadership not just from small processors, but from Extension agents, VDACS, elected officials, Virginia Tech, Farm Bureau... Right now, that isn't happening."
- Some producers were leaving direct marketing "even though 2020 was the best year they have ever had—because they can't find adequate processing in 2021."

## 2. "UPDATED-T&E Meats Covid-19 Program" (2020-06-11), /covid-19-response/

- "We are now overbooking our calendar approximately 20% to get as much meat through as fast as we can."
- Services suspended temporarily: link and patty sausage (loose sausage only), ground beef patties, Frenching, thin-sliced product, "Modern Boneless Cut beef", kabobs/fajita/tri-tip/flat iron/mock tender, **dry-aging**. Beef was cut "in the approximate order in which it was slaughtered", with a maximum of one cut sheet per carcass (still split 2-4 ways). Lamb and goat bookings were suspended until after June 10, 2020.
- [inferred] The constraint was cut-and-wrap labor and cooler time, not kill-floor capacity. The suspended items are all labor-heavy fabrication tasks, and dry-aging ties up cooler space.

## 3. Services page (current as of 2026-10-02 fetch)

- USDA-inspected slaughter and processing "for farmers in Virginia, West Virginia, and Maryland". Also custom (not-for-sale) processing for whole, half, and quarter sales. The plant does beef and pork, with "limited processing of small ruminants and exotics." Its specialty is private-label co-packing.
- "We process well over 1 million pounds of meat per year."
- Beef hangs "one to two weeks."
- Scheduling: "Typically, our busy months at T&E Meats run from May through December, so lead times for your animals during those months tends to be longer, especially between Labor Day and Thanksgiving... From January through April, we can more easily accommodate shorter lead times." Booking is by phone (540-434-4415) or web form. The form's timeframe choices are "As soon as possible / Within a few weeks / Later in the year."
- Pricing is "reviewed and updated annually." The fee sheet is in the companion data file `2026-10-02-r2-capacity-valley-processor-prices.md`.

## Relevance [inferred]

- The busiest window (Labor Day to Thanksgiving) at the largest USDA plant in the central Valley is when a mobile or custom-exempt unit could pick up overflow in Frederick County.
- Neither page gives a current (2025-26) lead time in weeks. The 16-month figure is the 2020 peak, not the steady state.
