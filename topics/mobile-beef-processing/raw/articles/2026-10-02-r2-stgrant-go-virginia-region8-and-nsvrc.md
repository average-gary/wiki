## Region 8 basics (DHCD)
- **Localities:** Augusta, Bath, Clarke, Frederick, Highland, Page, Rockbridge, Rockingham, Shenandoah, and Warren counties. The cities of Buena Vista, Harrisonburg, Lexington, Staunton, Waynesboro, and Winchester.
- **Snapshot:** population 556,612; average annual wage $53,547.
- **Targeted industries:** "Agriculture and Value-Added Food", Transportation and Logistics, Manufacturing, Life Sciences, IT and Emerging Technologies.
- **Support organization:** Northern Shenandoah Valley Regional Commission, led by Brandon Davis. Region contact: Alison Varner-Denbigh, Alison@govirginiaregion8.org.

## Program rules (Program Manual, July 2026)
- **"Regional activity" (§ 2.2-2489):** the project must be endorsed by the Regional Council, aligned with the Growth and Diversification Plan, and "executed by two or more localities, political subdivisions, or public bodies".
- **Single-business ban:** "GO Virginia is not an economic development incentive program and may not be used to provide direct or exclusive benefits to a single entity, or to fund activities that would directly support the operations, revenue, or growth of a single for-profit entity."
- **Eligible applicants:** "Local government entities or organizations working on behalf of the participating local government entities". This includes PDCs, nonprofits, regional economic development organizations, chambers, and colleges. A nonprofit may not apply for a project that "primarily or directly benefits an affiliated for-profit entity."
- **Funding pools:** Regional Per Capita funds, allocated by population share, and Competitive funds. Investment strategies are Talent Development, Sites and Infrastructure, Innovation and Entrepreneurship, and Cluster Scale Up.
- **Match:** "a 2:1 total match", meaning $1 of non-state match for every $2 of GO Virginia money. For Regional Implementation grants, "at least 20% of the match provided by local government units" (waiver possible).
- **Planning grants:** "Each region may allocate up to $250,000 per fiscal year toward Planning Grants. Requests under $100,000 can be administratively approved by DHCD." Applicants "should first contact their regional support organization."
- **No operating support:** funds must support new or expanded efforts, not ongoing operating costs.

## Shenandoah Valley Meat Processing Cooperative (Region 8 award)
- **Funded-projects list:** "ShenValley Meat Processing Cooperative", applicant Page County, Region 8, Cluster Scale-Up, approved 4/11/2025, $87,450.
- **Region 8 page:** "GO Virginia Funding $87,450, Strategic Framework Planning Grant", target industry Agriculture.
  - "establishing a state-of-the-art meat processing facility in Page County, Virginia ... By addressing critical bottlenecks in the aging, storage, and fabrication processes that currently hinder existing facilities, the project aims to triple the region's cattle processing capacity."
- **Deliverables:**
  1. Cooperative structure and governance.
  2. Partner relationships for processing steps the facility will not handle, including live receiving.
  3. Public-private legal agreements.
  4. Generic facility designs (coolers, cold storage, cattle receiving).
  5. A sustainability strategy, including additional funders and private investment.
- The DHCD description adds that it will serve "GO Virginia Region 8, as well as other meat processors statewide."
- **Other Valley-adjacent funded projects:** Clarke County Camp 7 Site Development Planning (Region 8). Region 9 has a food and beverage accelerator, Venture Central ($100,000, 1/8/2025).

## NSVRC
- NSVRC provides "Project Development, Grant writing, Procurement, Contract Management, Fiscal Agency", maintains the regional Comprehensive Economic Development Strategy (CEDS, updated every 5 years), and supports GO Virginia.
- **Southeast Crescent Regional Commission (SCRC):** in the NSV region, "Frederick, Clarke, and Winchester are designated as 'Attainment Counties'; Shenandoah, Warren, and Page are designated as 'Transitional Counties'; and the City of Winchester has a single isolated area of distress." Virginia received about $2.3M of SCRC's $20M in FY24. SCRC priorities include "Foster entrepreneurial and business development activities."
- No NSVRC business loan or grant open directly to private firms was found on the page.

## Implications for a Frederick County mobile beef startup [inferred]
- **No direct grant:** a for-profit startup cannot receive GO Virginia money directly. Two routes remain:
  1. Partner with or join the Page County cooperative effort, which is already funded and designing capacity for aging, storage, and fabrication. A mobile slaughter unit feeding carcasses into a Page County cut-and-wrap facility is a natural complement. Ask NSVRC whether live receiving or slaughter is one of the "uncompleted processing steps" the cooperative plans to outsource.
  2. Persuade Frederick County together with Clarke, Warren, or Winchester, through NSVRC, to apply for a regional planning grant, for example a mobile-slaughter feasibility study covering the northern counties. That would need a $1 non-state match for every $2 requested.
- **Ask NSVRC:** whether the cooperative's business plan, due roughly 2026, is public, and whether it treats a mobile unit as a feeder.
- **SCRC:** Attainment status means Frederick County projects get the lowest SCRC priority. Page and Shenandoah counties rank higher.
