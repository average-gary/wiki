# MSU Case Studies

## National mobile units (all USDA-inspected)

| Unit | Where | Years | Ownership | Volume | Beef fee | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Island Grown Farmers Cooperative** | San Juan Is., WA; cut plant in Bow | 2002– | Built by Lopez Community Land Trust; service co-op bought it; 30 founders paid $600 each | 9–10 beef/day, 3–4 days/wk; >300k lb in 2007; ~$500k/yr revenue | $105 + $0.82/lb (2008); $159 non-member (2022) | Operating as of 2018 page |
| **Puget Sound Meat Producers Co-op** | South Puget Sound, WA | 2009–2021 | Pierce Conservation District owns it and holds the loan; co-op leases for $1/yr | 516 animals (2010), 850 (2011) | $110 member / $140 non-member | Near-bankruptcy; closed by Jan 2021; Heritage Meats runs the unit parked ($216/hd, 2022) |
| **Coast Grown / CCAC** | Central Coast, CA | built 2002, inspected 2009 | Ranchers | 32 head in first 9 days | — | Parked 7 years |
| **Taos Mobile Matanza (TCEDC)** | Taos, NM | 2006, inspected May 2008 | County economic development corporation | — | — | Ran MSU plus 5,000 sf food center; status now unknown |
| **Nevada (UNR study)** | Silver Springs, NV | 2007 | Planned: 2 MSUs + fixed plant | 240 days/unit (assumed) | — | Never built |
| **Rusted Gate Farm** | Central Point, OR | 2022 plan | 501(c)(3) demonstration farm (Alumbra / Walton-funded) | 500–1,000 head planned | $175 planned; $240 needed | Decided not to buy |
