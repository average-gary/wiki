# Insurance Benchmarks

> **Confidence: low.** No current Virginia rate was found. The figures are from 2004–2013 templates.
> Use them only to make sure the pro forma includes a line, not to set its size.

## Workers' compensation class

- **NCCI 2081 Butchering** covers livestock handling, the kill floor, dressing, boning, chilling, fresh cuts and incidental sausage.
  A shop doing steaks, roasts, grind and sausage is assigned **2081**.
- **2089 Packing House** applies only when the business's *main* operation is further processing (curing, smoking, bacon,
  consumer packaging). The two can't be split within one business unless the operations really are separate
  ([NCCI scopes](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-ins-ncci-scopes-2081-2089.md)).
- Example rate (Iowa, 2009): **$10.89 per $100 payroll** for 2081, against $3.37 for 2089
  ([NMPAN](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-rig-nmpan-workers-comp-butchering-codes.md)).
  On $120k of payroll that would be about $13k/yr [inferred, applying the 2009 Iowa rate].
- **Virginia loss costs for 2081/2089: not found.** Ask the Bureau of Insurance or an agent.
- Private carriers may refuse workers' comp for start-ups, and payroll is audited every year (Penn State).

## Coverage checklist (Penn State + model) [partly inferred]

| Cover | Why |
|---|---|
| Workers' comp (2081) | Required with employees; the most expensive meat code |
| General liability | Working on customers' farms, with outside people and animals present |
| Product liability / recall | Premium based on sales and owner experience (Penn State) |
| Commercial auto + **cargo** | A vehicle policy doesn't cover animals or carcasses in a truck or trailer unless they are listed |
| Inland marine / equipment | The trailer and cutting equipment |
| **Spoilage** (refrigeration breakdown, power loss) | Penn State's "Temperature Sensitive Farm Personal Property" endorsement; critical for a cooler holding 20–30 owners' carcasses |
| **Bailee / customers' goods** | The carcasses belong to customers. No published premium found |
