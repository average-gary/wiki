# Grant and Financing Opportunities

> **The pattern.** Of 25+ programs checked, none gives grant money directly to a new for-profit
> mobile slaughter business. VAFAIRS (the Farm Bureau's business-assistance arm) says it plainly:
> "Very few grant programs help cover the cost of construction or equipment ... Most land,
> equipment, and construction projects require a loan."
> ([VAFAIRS](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-stgrant-va-financing-guide-vafairs-private-funders.md))
>
> Grants do matter for three things: a **feasibility study** paid for by a locality,
> **gap equipment** (≤$50k), and **expansion after year 1**. All program status is as of 2026-10-02.

## Deadlines on the calendar

| Date | Program | Who applies | What it could pay for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oct 30, 2026** | VDACS **AFID Infrastructure** | A county, city, EDA or PDC | ≤$50k reimbursement for special-purpose equipment or a cooler serving multiple small producers |
| **Nov 6, 2026, noon EST** | **Southern SARE Producer Grant** (2027 cycle) | A farmer ($20k) or farmer group ($25k) with ≥$1,000 farm income | **Research only**, e.g. an on-farm harvest study using a *rented* unit. Not the business, not equipment |
| Oct 16, 2026 | **REAP** new rule takes effect | A business operating ≥12 months | 25% of an energy project, e.g. solar or efficiency for a **fixed** cooler at the base farm. Trailer-mounted systems are excluded |
| Jan 31, 2027 | Agriculture Innovation Centers (AIC) | Centers that assist producers, not the business | Indirect: technical help |
| Rolling | **AFID Planning Grant** | One locality ($20k) or several ($35k) | A regional processing or mobile-unit **feasibility study** |
| Rolling, via NSVRC | **GO Virginia Region 8** planning grant | ≥2 localities or public bodies | A regional study; 2:1 match. Requests under $100k are approved administratively |
| ~Feb–Apr 2027 [inferred from FY2026] | **VAPG** | A **producer or producer group** owning >50% of the cattle | Planning ≤$50k; working capital ≤$200k |
| ~Spring 2027 [inferred] | **LFPP** / RFSP | Agricultural businesses, co-ops, networks | Planning $25k–$100k; implementation $100k–$500k; 25% match |
| Unverified | AFBF **Ag Innovation Challenge** | Startups | Up to $100k prize |
| Paused, "expected to return in 2026" | VAFAIRS **Bull Pen** challenge | Virginia ag startups | ~$30k (2023 figure) |

## Grants by route

### Route 1: through a locality (state money)

AFID is the state's main agricultural development fund. The 2026–28 budget deposits **$1.5M/yr**,
and there is **no dedicated meat-processing grant line** in any budget from 2022 to 2028
([budget](../../raw/data/2026-10-02-r2-stgrant-va-budget-meat-lines-and-tobacco-region.md)).

| AFID program | Size | Match | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Infrastructure** | ≤$50k, reimbursement | 1:1; **2:1** if distressed or serving multiple small producers | Best near-term fit. "Animal slaughtering facilities" are a named priority, and a mobile oyster barge set the precedent in 2022. **Confirm a slaughter trailer is "special purpose" and not an unallowable "general use motor vehicle".** ([AFID infra](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-mkt-vdacs-afid-infrastructure-grants.md)) |
| **Planning** | $20k / $35k multi-locality; capital ≤$10k | 1:1; ≥50% new cash | "Applications primarily benefiting an individual, for-profit business are not allowed". So the study has to be regional. Precedent: **Fauquier's $20k abattoir feasibility study (Oct 2022)** ([AFID planning/facility](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-stgrant-afid-planning-and-facility-grants.md)) |
| **Facility** | Lesser of $500k or 25% of capital investment | Locality matches $1:$1 | Paid **after** performance at 36 months, split ⅓ VA purchases, ⅓ jobs, ⅓ capital, with clawback. A 2–4 job startup scores poorly. Fits a later fixed cut-and-wrap build |

**The cooler is the natural AFID Infrastructure project (round 3)** [inferred]. Under the user's model the fixed cooler is exactly the kind of item AFID has funded before: Piedmont Processing's $25k walk-in freezer for "insufficient cooler space for aging beef" (2022). A cooler is unambiguously special-purpose equipment, which sidesteps the vehicle question. REAP could later fund efficiency or solar for it, since it is a fixed installation. The state's own five-year plan (RD894) makes AFID Planning and Infrastructure grants its Phase 1 tools, recommends a VDACS meat-processor development specialist, and backs custom-exempt capacity investment ([RD894](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-vastudy-vdacs-rd894-five-year-meat-capacity-plan.md)). Fauquier went back for a second, $9,800, AFID planning grant in Nov 2024, which shows sequential planning grants are normal ([Fauquier](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-vastudy-fauquier-livestock-exchange-abattoir-afid.md)).

The **Tobacco Region Revitalization Commission** does not cover any Valley locality. Frederick County and
Winchester are ineligible.

### Route 2: regional (GO Virginia Region 8 / NSVRC)

- GO Virginia "may not be used ... to fund activities that would directly support the operations,
  revenue, or growth of a single for-profit entity". It needs ≥2 localities and a 2:1 match
  ([GO Virginia](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-stgrant-go-virginia-region8-and-nsvrc.md)).
- **The ShenValley Meat Processing Cooperative.** On 4/11/2025, Region 8 granted Page County
  **$87,450** for a business plan for a cooperative meat processing facility. Its aim is to
  "triple the region's cattle processing capacity" by fixing **aging, storage, and fabrication**
  bottlenecks. It plans **partner relationships for processing steps it will not handle, including
  live receiving**. This is the single most important lead in this article: a mobile kill unit is a
  natural supplier of carcasses to it. See [[fixed-facility-dependency|The Fixed-Facility Dependency]] ([The Fixed-Facility Dependency](../concepts/fixed-facility-dependency.md)).
- Contacts: NSVRC (Brandon Davis); Region 8 (Alison Varner-Denbigh, Alison@govirginiaregion8.org).
- The Southeast Crescent Regional Commission rates Frederick, Clarke and Winchester as "Attainment" counties, its lowest priority.
  Page, Shenandoah and Warren are "Transitional" counties and rank higher.

### Route 3: federal, as a business

| Program | Status | Why it does or doesn't fit |
|---|---|---|
| **MPPEP** | Phase 4 (beef) closed Aug 7, 2026. Only a catfish Phase 3 is open (to Dec 7). No FY2027 beef notice | Needs **≥1 year operating under FSIS or equal-to state inspection**. A custom-exempt year doesn't count ([MPPEP](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-mkt-usda-mppep-phase-4.md)) |
| **REAP** | Rebuilt Oct 2026: applications are post-completion, with a 25% grant | Business must be ≥12 months old. The rule's own ineligible example is **solar on a trailer**, and vehicle efficiency is ineligible ([REAP](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-fedgrant-reap-2026-final-rule.md)). Medium confidence: a single source published the day before this was written |
| **LFPP / RFSP** | FY2026 closed Jun 5 | Fits businesses that market other producers' food. A pure custom-exempt service, where the meat can't be sold, fits poorly. RFSP funded only 6.5% of applicants in FY2025 ([LFPP](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-fedgrant-ams-lfpp-rfsp-nifa-bfrdp-2026.md)) |
| **VAPG** | FY2026 closed Apr 22 | Applicant must own >50% of the raw commodity, so only a **producer-owned** model qualifies |
| **RFSI** | Dead end | Explicitly excludes meat and poultry. Virginia's only round closed 2024 ([RFSI/SARE](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-fedgrant-vdacs-rfsi-and-southern-sare-producer.md)) |
| **MPIRG**, **MPILP** | Dormant / page removed | — |
| **RBDG** | FY2026 closed Jun 30 | Public bodies and nonprofits only. It can capitalize a **revolving loan fund** that lends to startups ([RD](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-fedgrant-usda-rd-rbdg-rmap-aic-rcdg-2026.md)) |
| **RMAP** | FY2026 closed Jun 30 | A startup with ≤10 FTEs can **borrow ≤$50k** (≤75% of project cost) from a nonprofit microlender that holds RMAP funds. Which Virginia lenders hold them is unknown |

### Route 4: private

- **VAFAIRS:** no grants. It offers free or low-fee feasibility studies, business plans and VAPG application help. This is a cheap way to get the plan written.
- **Community Foundation of the Northern Shenandoah Valley:** nonprofits only.
- **PEC Buy Fresh Buy Local:** marketing only, and its chapters don't cover Frederick County.
- **Farm Aid:** not verified.

## Debt: how the trailer actually gets bought

| Lender | Fit | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| **Farm Credit of the Virginias** | **Best fit.** FCA rules let it lend to a "farm-related service" business for "capital structures, equipment, and initial working capital" (12 CFR 613.3020). A processor is **cleanly eligible if farmers own >50%** (613.3010). It also leases equipment | FarmLaunch for young, beginning and veteran farmers: flexible underwriting, up to $500 off origination fees, can pair with FSA guarantees. Nearest branches: Leesburg, Warrenton, Harrisonburg. **Whether Frederick County is in FCV's territory is unconfirmed** ([FCV](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-lending-farm-credit-virginias-processing-eligibility.md)) |
| **VSBFA Cash Collateral** (SSBCI 2.0) | Covers the discount lenders apply to a custom trailer as collateral | Deposits up to the lesser of 40% of the bank loan or $1M; fees temporarily waived ([VSBFA](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-lending-vsbfa-loan-and-credit-support-programs.md)) |
| **VSBFA Capital Connect** (SSBCI 2.0) | Subordinate piece alongside a bank loan | Lesser of $1M or 40% of project; 25-yr amortization, <10-yr term; personal guarantee from >20% owners. SSBCI money is time-limited |
| **VSBFA Microloan** | **Year 2, not launch.** Business must be "currently operating" | Up to $150k, 5–7 yrs, 650+ credit score |
| **FSA Operating Loans** | **Barred for a standalone slaughter service.** A business mainly processing others' animals is a "non-eligible enterprise" (7 CFR 764.102(f)) | Open if a cattle farmer buys the rig mainly for their own herd (764.251), or if producers use OL money to buy stock in a cooperative that owns the unit ([FSA](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-lending-fsa-farm-loan-eligibility-non-eligible-enterprise.md)) |
| **SBA 7(a) / 504 / Microloan; USDA B&I** | Generic | Per the 2023 VDACS guide only: 7(a) ≤$5M; 504 needs ≥10% equity; SBA microloan ≤$50k; B&I needs 20% equity for new businesses and a rural location. Not researched further |

**Conflict recorded: VSBFA terms.** The 2023 VDACS guide gives Cash Collateral as the lesser of
$500k or 40%, and the Microloan as $25k needing **two years** in business. The live 2026 VSBFA pages
and 2024 fact sheets give a $1M cap for Cash Collateral and $150k for the Microloan, needing the
business to be "currently operating". This article uses the **2026/2024** figures as the newer
primary source. Confirm with VSBFA (804-786-6585).

## Structure decides which doors open [inferred]

| Ownership structure | Opens | Closes |
|---|---|---|
| **Sole for-profit operator** | FCV via 613.3020 (if eligible); VSBFA; equipment leasing; AFID Infrastructure *only if a locality sponsors it* | FSA OL, VAPG, LFPP fit, GO Virginia, all AFID Planning |
| **Producer cooperative or farmer-majority LLC** | FCV via 613.3010 (clean); **FSA OL for members buying co-op stock**; **VAPG**; LFPP; CFNSV if a nonprofit partner is involved; Southern SARE group grant ($25k research) | Simplicity: governance has been a documented failure mode (PSMPC; see [[msu-failure-modes|Why Mobile Slaughter Units Fail]] ([Why Mobile Slaughter Units Fail](../concepts/msu-failure-modes.md))) |
| **Supplier to the ShenValley co-op** | Its planning is already GO Virginia-funded; it plans to outsource live receiving | Independence; its location (Page County) and timeline are outside your control |

## A realistic funding stack for year 1 [inferred, not costed]

1. **Trailer:** a Farm Credit term loan or lease, with VSBFA Cash Collateral if the lender
   discounts the collateral. The custom-exempt rig is $72k–$115k (Sentinel 2026 list), not
   $550k. See [[custom-exempt-mobile-rig|Custom-Exempt Mobile Rig]] ([Custom-Exempt Mobile Rig](../concepts/custom-exempt-mobile-rig.md)).
2. **Plan:** VAFAIRS feasibility study and business plan (free or low fee).
3. **Study, through the county:** ask Frederick County (alone, or with Clarke and Warren through NSVRC) to file
   an AFID Planning Grant for a northern-Valley processing and mobile-unit study, modeled on Fauquier 2022.
4. **Gap equipment:** AFID Infrastructure (≤$50k) through a locality, for example a cooler at a partner cut
   shop. That **misses Oct 30, 2026** unless a county is already on board, so target the spring 2027 round.
5. **Year 2+:** VSBFA Microloan; REAP for a fixed cooler; MPPEP only if moving to inspection.
