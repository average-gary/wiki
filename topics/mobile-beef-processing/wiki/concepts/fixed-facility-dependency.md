# The Fixed-Facility Dependency

> "Every MSU must partner with a fixed facility ('cut and wrap') where further processing is
> done." (NMPAN MSU Manual, 2010)

## Why mobility doesn't remove the plant

- The unit's cooler holds carcasses for about 24 hours.
- Beef then hangs at about 0 °C for "a minimum of seven days, although 14-21 days is more common", before
  cutting ([Nevada 2007](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-econ-nevada-msu-fixed-fabrication-facility-2007.md)).
  Rail space must therefore hold **1–3 weeks of kill volume**.
- Cutting, packaging, freezing and customer pickup all happen at the fixed site.

## Evidence it is the binding constraint

| Case | What happened |
|---|---|
| IGFC (WA) | Expanding the 3,000 sf Bow plant nearly doubled hanging capacity, and it was maxed out again. The co-op limited new members because of space and staff ([IGFC](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-case-island-grown-farmers-cooperative.md)). |
| Taos Mobile Matanza (NM) | Ended up running **two** inspected facilities (MSU plus a 5,000 sf food center). The state rejected on-site wastewater treatment and required a sewer hookup ([NMPAN manual](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-risk-nmpan-msu-manual-lessons.md)). |
| Nevada (2007) | Fixed plant costed at $1.51M (later revised to about $0.8M for the building) against about $207k per MSU. Never built. |
| Rusted Gate (OR, 2022) | The plan warns that partner cut-and-wrap plants need "the time, space and labor" for the summer and fall surge ([Rusted Gate](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-econ-rusted-gate-msu-business-plan-2022.md)). |
| Piedmont Processing (Orange, VA) | Growth "currently limited by insufficient cooler space for aging beef". Received a $25k AFID grant for a walk-in freezer in 2022 ([AFID](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-mkt-vdacs-afid-infrastructure-grants.md)). |
| Blue Ridge Meats (Middletown, VA) | Books 3–5 months out. Whether that queue is kill-floor or cooler/cutting capacity is **unknown** ([Blue Ridge](../../raw/data/2026-10-02-econ-blue-ridge-meats-frederick-county-terms.md)). |
| 7 USDA plants, northern VA (PEC/AFT 2021) | Running at 125–150% of capacity, "stressed by storage limitations and labor needs". **Every** plant named meat-cutter shortage as its main constraint ([PEC](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-capacity-pec-fuller-processor-study-2021.md)). |
| T&E Meats (Harrisonburg, 2020) | When demand hit, it **dropped labor-heavy cutting** (patties, links, specialty cuts, dry-aging), not kill slots ([T&E](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-capacity-te-meats-covid-backlog.md)). |
| Vermont (2014, peer-reviewed) | Cold storage and cut-and-wrap were tighter bottlenecks than slaughter ([Gwin & Thiboumery](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-r2-lit-gwin-thiboumery-2014-institutional-entrepreneurship.md)). |

## Three ways to solve it

1. **Partner with an existing cut shop.** This needs the lowest capital. The risk is that MSU
   carcasses compete with the partner's own customers for the same scarce rail space, especially
   in the fall. In Virginia, custom-exempt rules expressly allow one operator to slaughter and a
   different one to process ([VDACS](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-reg-vdacs-red-meat-custom-exemptions.md)).
2. **Build or lease your own cooler and cut room.** This is the expensive line item. A
   refrigerated trailer or walk-in cooler is the kind of equipment a $50k AFID grant can fund.
3. **Join an existing butcher business as its added kill capacity.** This is the PSMPC
   survival model.

**The diligence question for the Shenandoah plan** [inferred]: is the incumbents' backlog on the
kill floor or in the cooler? **Round 2 evidence points to the cooler and the cutters** (PEC, T&E, Vermont). A mobile kill unit then adds carcasses to a queue that is already full, unless it is paired with cutting capacity.

**4. Feed the ShenValley Meat Processing Cooperative** (round 2). GO Virginia Region 8 granted Page County $87,450 (2025) to plan a facility for exactly the **aging, storage, and fabrication** steps, partnering out "live receiving". A mobile kill unit is the missing upstream piece ([GO Virginia](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r2-stgrant-go-virginia-region8-and-nsvrc.md)). Timing and location (Page County) are outside the operator's control.

## The user's model brings it in-house (round 3)

The chosen configuration owns the cooler and does the cutting on the mobile unit, docked at the cooler. That removes the partner dependency, but the binding resources (cooler rail and cutter hours) become the operator's own constraint.
