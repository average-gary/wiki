# Cooler Site Permits

> **Bottom line [inferred].** A cooler that only stores carcasses might pass as a farm use. A cooler where
> a mobile unit **docks to cut** is a meat-processing site: a CUP in RA plus a wastewater solution.
> Get a written **use determination** from the Frederick County Zoning Administrator (§ 165-101.06)
> before buying land or equipment.

## Zoning: Frederick County Ch. 165, RA district

Read from Wayback snapshots dated 2026-07-11; the code's last amendment was 2026-05-13
([zoning](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-coolreg-frederick-county-zoning-ra.md)).

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is a slaughterhouse allowed by right? | **No.** Conditional use **BB "Slaughterhouses"** (§ 165-401.03) |
| What counts as a slaughterhouse? | "Establishments primarily engaged in the slaughtering **or processing** of meats for human consumption." § 165-204.17A reaches any establishment where animals "dead or alive, are processed" |
| Can it be agricultural storage by right? | Only storage "in its unmanufactured state" **and** only if the farm "regularly produces more than 1/2 of the commodity so treated". A service cooler for other owners fails this |
| Cold storage or warehouse? | **Not a listed RA use.** It would mean rezoning to a business or industrial district |
| CUP conditions (§ 165-204.17) | Buildings, unloading and pens ≥ **100 ft** from property lines; total buildings ≤ **20,000 sq ft**; **all operations under roof**, screened from neighbours and roads; 14-gauge screens; extra buffering at the administrator's discretion |
| Process | Planning Commission and Board of Supervisors public hearings, plus a site plan |
| Wastewater hint | RA restaurants (CUP E) must have an approved drainfield, and "pump and hauls are prohibited" |

**Siting options** [inferred]:
1. **RA parcel with a CUP.** Budget for the hearings, the setbacks, and a roofed dock bay so the trailer's cutting is
   "under roof".
2. **Business- or industrially zoned parcel with public sewer.** This avoids the RA questions and solves wastewater.
3. **Cooler on the operator's own farm, storing mostly the farm's own beef.** This fits by-right agriculture but defeats the
   service model.

**Not checked:** how Shenandoah and Clarke counties zone this. Siting outside Frederick County may be easier.

## Building code

- Va. Code **§ 36-99** exempts farm buildings from the Uniform Statewide Building Code. The exceptions are
  on-farm restaurants, flood-plain rules, and agritourism buildings open to the public.
- **§ 36-97** lists "handling, processing ... of agricultural animal products" as a farm-building use, with **no**
  "produced on the farm" limit (the crop use has one). That gives a textual argument for exempting a carcass cooler
  on a working farm ([§ 36-99](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-coolreg-va-code-36-99-farm-building-exemption.md)).
- The exemption does **not** apply off-farm, and the local building official decides. Zoning, VDACS and VDH rules
  apply regardless.

## Sanitation: 9 CFR 416.1–416.6 for the cooler

These are performance standards, not a construction spec
([416](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-coolreg-9cfr-416-sanitation.md)):
- Walls, floor and ceiling made of durable materials impervious to moisture. Openings that keep vermin out. Ventilation that controls condensation.
- **No inedible material in the carcass cooler** (416.2(b)(4)). Carcasses protected while loading and unloading (416.4(d)).
- **Private well:** potability documented, "renewed at least semi-annually" (416.2(g)(1)). **Private sewage:** a
  health-authority approval letter on request (416.2(f)).
- Written sanitation SOPs (416.11–17) are federally required only of inspected plants.
- In Virginia, 2VAC5-210 adopts these via the federal rule text as of Jan 1, 2021. **Still unknown:** whether OMPS inspects the
  cooler as part of the Custom Permit.

## Water supply

A small cooler with a mobile crew is far below VDH waterworks thresholds (12VAC5-590): ≥15 connections or ≥25 people
for ≥60 days a year; a nontransient system needs 25 of the same people for 6+ months. **A private well is fine**, with the
semi-annual coliform test above. The one risk is customer pickup bringing 25+ people a day
([12VAC5-590](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-coolreg-12vac5-590-waterworks-thresholds.md)).

## Wastewater at the dock

| Route | Rule | Fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Public sewer (Frederick Water)** | Excluded from DEQ permits (9VAC25-32-40(1)). Grease trap may be required by county building inspections; pretreatment plans need DEQ approval. **No stormwater to sewer**, so the wash-down pad must be roofed or bermed. 2025 rates: $50.18/month base (¾-in. meter) + $7.03–11.06 per 1,000 gal. 2019-era fees: $3,076 availability + $3,500 lateral | **Best** ([Frederick Water](../../raw/data/2026-10-02-r3-water-frederick-water-sewer-specs-fees.md)) |
| **VDH-permitted onsite system (AOSS)** | Must be custom-engineered and PE-sealed "for the anticipated receiving wastewater characteristics". The design-flow table has no meat category; over 1,000 gpd is a Large AOSS. A staffed building also needs a separate domestic system | Possible, but costly ([VDH](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-water-vdh-12vac5-610-613-onsite-sewage.md)) |
| **Pump-and-haul** | VDH permit route (12VAC5-610-410 to 440) | Frederick County bans it for RA restaurants, so expect resistance |
| **Land application** | Needs a **DEQ VPA permit**. Blood and wash water are "industrial wastes"; no general permit for small meat processors | Hard ([VPA](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-water-9vac25-32-vpa-scope-exclusions.md)) |

Volume and strength are covered in [[slaughter-waste-disposal-virginia|Slaughter Waste Disposal in Virginia]] ([Slaughter Waste Disposal in Virginia](slaughter-waste-disposal-virginia.md)).

## Calls

1. **Frederick County Zoning Administrator:** use determination for a cooler where a mobile cut unit docks. Also get a CUP timeline and cost.
2. **Frederick Water / FWSA:** sewer availability at candidate parcels, discharge limits, and any high-strength surcharge.
3. **Frederick County building official:** would a cooler on a working farm get the § 36-97 exemption?
4. **VDACS OMPS:** does the Custom Permit cover the docked cut room and the cooler, and is there a site inspection?
5. **Shenandoah and Clarke county zoning offices:** comparison.
