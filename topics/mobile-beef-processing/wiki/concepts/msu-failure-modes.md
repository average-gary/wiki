# Why Mobile Slaughter Units Fail

> **Evidence base: thin.** Six cases, mostly West Coast, mostly 2002–2013, all USDA-inspected.
> Documented shutdowns are under-reported. Round 2 added the ERS report ERR-150 (2013) and two
> peer-reviewed papers (2014, 2023), which corroborate the patterns but add no new shutdown
> cases. **No peer-reviewed US study of MSU economics was found.** Read this as a list of
> patterns, not base rates.

## Track record

| Unit | Outcome | Proximate cause |
|---|---|---|
| Nevada (two MSUs + plant, 2007) | **Never built** | — ([Nevada](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-econ-nevada-msu-fixed-fabrication-facility-2007.md)) |
| Rusted Gate (OR, 2022) | **Cancelled** at plan stage | No scenario broke even without grants and a $240/hd fee ([Rusted Gate](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-econ-rusted-gate-msu-business-plan-2022.md)) |
| Coast Grown (CA, built 2002) | **Parked 7 years**; 32 head in its first 9 operating days (2009) | "Regulatory complexities paired with uncertain markets" ([NMPAN manual](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-risk-nmpan-msu-manual-lessons.md)) |
| PSMPC (WA, 2009) | **Near-bankruptcy**, then closed by January 2021; unit now parked at a plant | Volume far below feasibility study; too many paid staff ([PSMPC](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-case-puget-sound-meat-producers-coop.md)) |
| Central Coast MHU (CA) | **Idle about 2 years**, then leased to J&R Meats (butcher shop) | No hanging or cut-and-wrap partner; 5–6 beef/day, 3 staff, $800+/day ([Central Coast](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-r3-cutshop-nmpan-central-coast-mhu.md)) |
| Taos Mobile Matanza (NM, 2006) | Operating as of 2010; current status unknown | Ended up running two inspected facilities |
| IGFC (WA, 2002) | **Operating**, at least as of the 2018 page | Members "have no other options for slaughter/processing" ([IGFC](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-case-island-grown-farmers-cooperative.md)) |

## The failure modes

1. **Volume below the feasibility study.** At PSMPC only about 20% of members used the unit
   regularly. Many lacked established meat markets, and record cattle prices made selling at
   auction more attractive. Rusted Gate projected only 1–2 kill days a week. The optimistic NMPAN 2011
   pro forma assumed 260 full days a year from launch
   ([NMPAN 2011](../../raw/data/2026-10-02-risk-nmpan-2011-msu-proforma-assumptions.md)).
2. **Seasonality.** Fall-heavy demand (Nevada). July–August is jammed with 4-H fair animals
   (Oregon). February–April is slow (IGFC sends staff on unpaid leave). The fixed cut-and-wrap
   peaks at the same time as the kill demand.
3. **Butcher labor.** Butchers at half-time are hard to recruit. A good cutter takes about 2 years to train.
   IGFC added health insurance in 2008 to keep staff. Labor is about 75% of cost.
4. **Management overhead.** PSMPC's "nearly a half-dozen paid staff" "did not prove
   sustainable". At IGFC the head butcher had no management experience, and scheduling (which is
   "critical to cash flow") fell to the board.
5. **The fixed facility.** See [[fixed-facility-dependency|The Fixed-Facility Dependency]] ([The Fixed-Facility Dependency](fixed-facility-dependency.md)).
6. **Offal, blood and wastewater.** Rules vary by state. California bans on-ranch composting
   and requires a renderer; New Mexico and Washington allow composting on the farm. Gut disposal
   was budgeted at $500/month in the NMPAN 2011 guide. It is Rusted Gate's third-largest cost.
   **Virginia (round 2):** on-farm composting is likely exempt. See [[slaughter-waste-disposal-virginia|Slaughter Waste Disposal in Virginia]] ([Slaughter Waste Disposal in Virginia](slaughter-waste-disposal-virginia.md)).
7. **Inspection friction** (inspected units only). FSIS warns that staffing mobile units "may present a
   challenge". Each farm stop needs its own health-authority wastewater letter, and inspector
   interpretations differ ([FSIS guide](../../raw/articles/2026-10-02-reg-fsis-msu-compliance-guide.md)).
8. **Regulatory overhead beyond FSIS.** A Class A CDL with DOT drug testing to tow the trailer,
   water potability testing at each site, state DOT terminal inspections (California), and
   food-defense key control across a co-op's members.
9. **Literature corroboration (round 2).** ERR-150 says MSUs may cost more per head than a fixed plant, and that PSMPC's use came in well below its feasibility studies. It notes some FSIS districts don't follow the guidance allowing outdoor kill and bleed-out. A 2023 Missouri survey ranks **labor** as the top processor concern (3.98/5), with financing last (1.56/5). New England work found **year-round animal supply** was the biggest problem, and **committed slaughter dates** were the documented fix for no-shows ([ERR-150](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-r2-lit-ers-err150-local-meat-processing-commitments.md), [Missouri](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-r2-lit-syukron-su-2023-missouri-small-processors.md), [Gwin & Thiboumery](../../raw/papers/2026-10-02-r2-lit-gwin-thiboumery-2014-institutional-entrepreneurship.md)).
10. **Subsidized competition.** In Oregon, fixed plants were expanding with "significant federal
   funding" at the same time Rusted Gate was planning.

## Inverted: what the survivors had

- **Capital they did not have to repay.** Grants and donations (IGFC), or a public owner leasing
  for $1/yr (PSMPC).
- **A captive membership with no alternative.** IGFC's members are islanders with no
  practical way to reach a mainland plant.
- **Fee discipline.** IGFC raised its fees within six months of opening and several times after.
- **Contract labor folded into an existing butcher business** (PSMPC's turnaround).

**Shenandoah reading** [inferred]: the Valley is not an island. Fixed USDA plants exist within
reach (five Very Small USDA cattle plants from Winchester to Harrisonburg; see [[valley-processor-landscape|Valley Processor Landscape]] ([Valley Processor Landscape](../references/valley-processor-landscape.md))), so the "no other option"
condition behind IGFC's success does not hold. The case for a mobile unit rests instead on
**booking lead time** and **hauling cost**, and both need local numbers.
